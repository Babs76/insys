## 1. Free cover fails safe (GAP-043)
- **What happened before this batch** when a scheme's size matched no band, or its band was not in force: there was no free cover limit, and **no limit meant no evidence was ever required**, at any cover.
- **Now** no limit means **evidence is required for any cover** (above zero), never "no limit".
- **A band check on submit:** a tenant's bands must start at 1 member and must not overlap or leave gaps (422 `LIFE_FREE_COVER_BANDS_INVALID`), checked against the other bands in force from the draft's start. Above the highest band no limit applies, so evidence is required (fail safe). Bands are therefore built up from 1 member, lowest first.
- **Tests** (`FreeCoverBandTests`):
  - no band matches the scheme's size;
  - the matching band is not yet in force;
  - an overlapping band refused;
  - a band leaving members below it uncovered refused;
  - a new version of a band with the same range accepted.

## 2. Tenant time zone (ADR-025)
- **Required at registration:** `timeZone`, an IANA name. A Windows name or an unknown zone is refused (422). It is stored on the tenant (V0035), and business dates use the locale defaults' zone first, then this one.
- **Existing tenants** without a time zone keep the Africa/Johannesburg default. `/health` check `tenant-time-zones` is Degraded and lists their codes in `data.tenantsWithoutTimeZone`, so they can be fixed.
- **`/identity/me`:** `tenant.timeZone` and `tenant.businessDate`.
- **Tests:** `TenancyTests.RegisterTenant_NeedsAnIanaTimeZone_ADR025` (refusals, stored zone, health listing) and `MeTests`.

## 3. OQ-079, decided and built
- **The rule:** a Life lump-sum claim gets its initial reserve at registration, equal to the sum assured of each claimed cover. "Lump sum" means the benefit type's `payout_type` is `LUMP_SUM`, which covers death and fixed-sum disability.
- **How:**
  - the reserve is set through the Claims Framework, once (a repeat registration leaves it);
  - it is on `Life:Claims:InitialReserveType` (`INDEMNITY` by default), with its basis recorded as the reason.
- **Adjustments:** every reserve move now needs a reason (`ClaimsMoveReserve`), which the audit trail records.
- **What I found:** the Claims Framework has **no reserve authority limit** today. The authority rules apply when a payment is approved and released, not when a reserve moves. The decision mentions existing reserve authority rules; there are none to apply, so I recorded this in OQ-079 rather than invent limits.
- **Test:** `LifeProcessTests` (end to end): the death claim starts with a reserve of the cover's 1,000,000.
- OQ-079 closed.
