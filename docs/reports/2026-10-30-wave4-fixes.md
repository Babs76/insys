## Health claims held for arrears are declined in the suspension's run (F006)
**Found by:** the front end's live run. A Health claim held for arrears was declined one daily-jobs run after its membership's suspension, not in the same run.

**Intended?** No. The renewals and retention addendum (Health grace) says that after `suspend_after_days` the membership is suspended for arrears "and held claims are declined with the scheme's arrears reason": one step, with no day between. `Membership.SuspendForArrears` documents the same.

**Cause.** `HealthJobs.Run` found all its due work in one query before doing any of it. The held claims to decline were chosen before that run's suspensions were recorded, so a claim whose membership the run suspended waited for the next run. The end-to-end test ran the job twice, which hid the lag.

**Fix.**
- The job now declines held claims after the run's other work, from a fresh query (`FindHeldClaimsToDecline`), so a claim is declined in the same run as its membership's suspension.
- `HealthDueWork` no longer carries the claims to decline.
- The end-to-end test `HealthProcessTests` (grace) runs the job once and expects both the suspension and the decline.
