## Payment callback: which token it needs (F5 finding 2)

**Today** the callback endpoint needs a **user's bearer token**: a Keycloak access token whose `sub` is an IAM user of the tenant holding `payment_callback.create`, with the tenant taken from its `tenant_id`. It also needs the `X-Signature` HMAC of the body. A provider cannot hold a user token. **Proposed fix (ADR-023):** OAuth 2.0 client credentials, one Keycloak client per tenant and provider, whose service account is an IAM service identity holding only `payment_callback.create`. This keeps the tenant coming from the authenticated principal (TEN-002). The signature stays, keyed per tenant and provider and covering a timestamp (5-minute window, against replay), with a WAF allow-list as defence in depth. I chose this over mTLS, because TLS ends at the ELB. I rejected signature-only, because the tenant would come from the URL.

## The operator tenant (F5 finding 3)

**Proposed rule (ADR-024):** business modules refuse the operator tenant with 403 `OPERATOR_TENANT_NOT_BUSINESS`, for reads and changes alike. Tenancy, Identity, Static data, Audit, Integration and Workflow stay open to it. It is enforced by one endpoint filter per business module, with a sweep test, and business jobs skip the operator tenant.

- **Other consumers' prerequisites:** only Payments' payouts throw `PrerequisiteMissingException` so far. Billing, Documents, Reinsurance and Analytics still fail and dead-letter as before when configuration is missing, but their failures now keep order per record. Each change is small. I propose doing them in the next batch, with a test each.
- **The platform operator's part of the second escalation** is the `/health` check and the error log (the Cloud Eye alarm), not a Workflow task: a consumer acts only in its event's tenant (TEN-004). This is recorded in ADR-022.
- **Global static data** published in the operator tenant does not retry other tenants' parked deliveries at once. They wait for the 15-minute periodic check or a manual retry (ADR-022).
