## Health products (OQ-080, built)
- **Plan options name their product.**
  - Submitting a plan option without one, or with a different product from its plan's other options, is refused (`HEALTH_PLAN_PRODUCT_INVALID`).
  - **One choice:** that the product is a Health product on sale is checked at enrolment and plan change, not on submit. A tenant sets up plans before it publishes products, and the static data check has no access to the Product Factory.
- **Enrolment and plan change** need the plan's product on sale (`HEALTH_PRODUCT_NOT_AVAILABLE`). The membership records it, and a plan change to another product needs that product in scope.
- **Product scopes now reach Health:** memberships, their claims, preauthorisations, cases and new Billing schedules carry the product.
- **Plans with no product yet:**
  - their memberships stay outside product scopes, as before;
  - `/health` lists them (`health-plan-products`);
  - the daily job gives the memberships the product once the plan's next version names one.
  - **A known limit:** their existing Billing schedules keep no product, so product-scoped users don't see those invoices (fail-safe). New enrolments are complete.
- **OQ-080** closes when this batch is merged.

## Health payment runs and remittances (F103, built)
- **A remittance pays many members' claims,** so it is in scope only when all its claims are, by their memberships' product and branch.
- **A partly-in-scope run** shows only the remittances in scope, with their count and total, and `partlyInScope`. A run with none in scope is not found, in the run list too.
- **Acting on a run needs the whole run in scope** (403 `OUT_OF_SCOPE`): cutting off, calculating and releasing. Calculating checks every claim ready for the run.
- **The run's approval** is scoped when all its claims share one product and branch; otherwise it is for checkers with unrestricted scope only.
- **The remittance's payment** (`HEALTH_REMITTANCE`) follows it in `/payments` and payout batches.
