# Wave 6 batch 6.3: fraud investigation and bulk jobs (F058, F076). Wave 6 is complete, with policy import open

- **Fraud cases (F058, OQ-160):** for Life and General, on a claim or a policy. Only holders of the fraud function reach them, and every read is audited, the list included. A case raises no event and has no portal route, so nothing reaches its subject. Its outcome changes nothing about a claim.
- **Bulk (F076):** individuals are imported all or nothing, each row through `RegisterParty`'s validator and duplicate match, and every bad line is named. An export of parties waits for a second person, leaves as CSV with no identifier numbers, and is audited with its filter and row count.

## Differs from the addendum
- **Fraud cases aren't a Workflow case family.** Cases notify their complainant and reopen claims when upheld, and portals read them. A separate aggregate, as Health's is, has none of those ways to tip someone off.
- **Bulk jobs live in Party**, because an import must be one transaction with the parties it creates.

## Decisions for Ade
1. **Policy import isn't built, though OQ-158(a) names it.** A row can't go through the API's commands as the addendum says: `IssuePolicy` needs a rated quote and raises billing, documents and commission, so it can't be all or nothing across modules, and a migrated policy's history needs back-dated rules. *Recommendation: build it with B-013, whose reconciliation rules it needs anyway; add about 8 to 12 days to B-013's estimate.*
2. **Organisations can't be imported yet,** only individuals. *Recommendation: add when a customer's data has them (1 day).*
3. **Policy exports aren't built.** The existing lists page through policies with their scope. *Recommendation: add with the policy import.*
4. **Automatic opening.** A fraud case is opened by a person, though an indicator's referral could open one as Health's does. *Recommendation: keep it manual until the indicators have been tuned.*
