## What a scope now does
A role assignment scoped to products or organisation nodes (`docs/specs/addenda/access-scopes.md`) limits what its permissions reach. A node covers every node below it.

| Operation | Outside the caller's scope for the endpoint's permission |
|---|---|
| Read one record | 404, as if it did not exist |
| Act on a record | 403 `OUT_OF_SCOPE` (new) |
| Lists | Filtered in the query, so paging stays correct |
| Create | The new record's product and node must be in scope (403 `OUT_OF_SCOPE`) |

- **Where it is checked:** once per module, in the repository that loads records and in the list queries, against the scope of the permission the endpoint requires. No endpoint has its own check.
- **Jobs and consumers** act as the service and are unrestricted.
- **No organisation structure:** a tenant without one has no organisation scoping; product scopes still apply.

## Which records

| Module | Carries the node | Scoped as its parent |
|---|---|---|
| General | Quotes, policies, claims (V0036) | Proposals (quote), endorsements (policy) |
| Life | Proposals, policies, claims, group schemes (V0037) | Underwriting cases (proposal), loans (policy) |
| Health | Memberships (V0038) | Preauthorisations, medical claims, utilisation cases, fraud cases about a claim |
| Claims Framework | The claim register, from the domain claim at registration (V0039) | Reserves, evidence, payment instructions |
| Billing | Schedules and invoices, from the policy-issued and member-enrolled events (V0041) | Accounts and refunds: in scope when any of the account's schedules is |
| Workflow | Approvals about any of the records above (V0040), from the owning module when requested | |

- **A new record's node:** the request's `orgNodeCode`, else the user's one node, else the root legal entity.
- **Records that follow:** policies, renewals, claims, Billing and approvals keep the node of the record they come from.
- **Existing records:** the migrations place them at the tenant's root legal entity, and copy it onward to claims, schedules, invoices and approvals.

## Two questions and one interim choice
- **OQ-080, Health has no product.**
  - Health schemes have no product, and memberships carry a plan, not a Product Factory product.
  - Built fail safe: Health is scoped by node only, and a grant restricted to products reaches no Health record.
  - The options are in the question.
- **OQ-081, two rules the addendum leaves open.**
  - **Accounts:** an account is in scope when any of its schedules is.
  - **Approvals:** the checker's scope for the approval permission they use.
  - Both are built as described; confirm or change them.
- **Interim control lifted.** Scoped role assignments are accepted, `/identity/me` reports `scopesEnforced: true`, and `SCOPES_NOT_ENFORCED` is gone.
  - The error catalogue only adds codes (SDM-002), so a database that already has the row keeps it as ACTIVE. The API no longer returns it.

## Also fixed
The request scope was set in the operator-tenant filter, which only business modules have. Workflow is not one of them, so its requests ran unrestricted. The scope is now set by a filter on every module's group.

## Tests
- **End to end:**
  - **General:** a region user lists, reads and acts only within its branches, and creates only there; a product-scoped user sees none of another product.
  - **Life:** the same for proposals and policies; the policy keeps its proposal's node, as do its Billing schedule and invoice (404 to the region user, 200 to an unscoped one).
  - **Health:** the same for memberships; a product-scoped user sees none (OQ-080).
- **Integration:**
  - **Claims Framework:** a registered claim's record, financials and instructions are 404 outside its product.
  - **Workflow:** a scoped approval is hidden from lists and reads, and refused with `OUT_OF_SCOPE`, outside the checker's scope; unscoped approvals are unaffected.
  - **Identity:** a scoped role assignment is accepted and listed; `/identity/me` reports enforcement.
