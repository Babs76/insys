- **Preventive:** a line whose code is on a schedule is checked against age, sex and interval. Within it, the line draws on the preventive category, with no co-payment if the schedule says so. Outside it, day-to-day benefits. Nothing goes to the member.
- **Order of categories on a line:** registered condition, then programme, then schedule, then the usual mapping.
