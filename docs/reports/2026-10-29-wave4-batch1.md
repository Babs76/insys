## Choices made in building it (none changes a signed-off decision)
- **Ade's corrections and decisions of 29 October 2026 are built** (OQ-137 below):
  - a General loss in grace is accepted and its payment held (my first build refused it, which changed agreed behaviour);
  - Health reinstatement on payment is a tenant setting, on by default.
- **Cover in grace decides the cancellation date.** With cover in grace, the policy is cancelled from the day after grace (as the grace notice said), so losses in grace stay covered and are paid as usual. Without it, the policy is cancelled from the first unpaid due date, and the hold above applies.
- **A deduction only when the settlement covers the arrears.** A settlement no larger than the arrears is held instead of netted, so a payment is never zero or partial against arrears that remain.
- **The short-period refund is from the annual premium**, as the pro rata refund is. Billing credits the unpaid instalments on cancellation as it already does.
- **The employer's contributions count** with the member's: either unpaid starts the member's grace.
- **A cancellation reason gains `short_period_scale`** on the existing table, as §3.1 proposed. The base static data catalogue entry gains the field, as earlier migrations to existing tables did.

## A decline for non-payment sends the rights notice (OQ-102)
**Confirmed and built.** A claim declined because grace ended unpaid sends `CLAIM_DECISION_RIGHTS` to the policyholder, with the decline reason's customer wording. Tested in `GeneralTests.ASettlementInGrace_…` and end to end in path 3.

Before this, `CLAIM_DECISION_RIGHTS` was sent only for a partial approval: a claim declined in full by staff (`DeclineClaim`) sends none. The notification type's own description covers a decision that "declines all or part". That is outside this batch and unchanged, and needs your call: should a full decline by staff send it too?
