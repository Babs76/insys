## Health product follow-ups
- **Billing follows an assigned product.**
  - When the daily job gives an older membership its plan's product, or a plan change moves it to another product, Health raises `MembershipProductAssigned`, and Billing gives the membership's schedules (member's and employer's) the product.
  - Invoices raised from then on carry it; those already raised keep what they had.
  - **Tested:** the schedule takes the product; a later invoice carries it; a user scoped to the product sees that invoice, and one scoped to another product does not.
- **`/health` `health-plan-products`** now lists plan options whose product is missing, is not a product the tenant has defined, is not a Health product, or differs from the plan's other options.
  - The domain comes from a new Product Factory query (`IProductDomains`), any status.
  - **Tested:** all four cases, and a correct plan not listed.

## External-user access (built)
- **Links (§1):**
  - `POST /identity/users/{id}/subject-link` links a user to a party, an intermediary (active and licensed) or a provider (active). One current link per user.
  - It is pending until a second person approves it (`EXTERNAL_SUBJECT_LINK`), and ended at once by `…/subject-link/end`.
  - The permission cache is dropped on each change, so the next request has it.
- **The gate (§5, §6),** on every module endpoint, for a linked user:
  - only endpoints marked for their portal (else `EXTERNAL_USER_NOT_ALLOWED`);
  - only through the channel of their audience (else `CHANNEL_NOT_ENABLED`);
  - and MFA where required (else `PORTAL_MFA_REQUIRED`).
  - **MFA:** brokers and providers always need it; members when the member portal client's authentication policy says so (new `client_ids` and `mfa_required` on the policy table). MFA is read from Keycloak's `amr` claim (otp, mfa, hwk, swk; the AMR mapper adds it) or an `acr` level of 2 or above.
- **Relationship scope (§2, §3),** checked with the F103 scope in the portal handlers and list queries:
  - **Members:** their policies (policyholder; a Life insured life too), their claims, their Health membership (principal or dependant).
  - **Brokers:** records with their intermediary code or a sub-broker's (hierarchy). They quote only for a client they service or registered, always under their own code.
  - **Providers:** their own claims, preauthorisations and remittances. They submit only as themselves; the provider comes from the link.
- **Portal views (§4)** are separate response types without reserves, rationale, fraud, underwriting, clinical review, billing internals or other parties. Life beneficiaries go to the policyholder only.
- **Party match (§6):**
  - a broker's registration that matches an existing party (identity document hash, or the same names and birth date) gets the same 202 "Received" answer as a new one;
  - the match opens a staff review task (`PARTY_MATCH_REVIEW`);
  - a matched document stays with the existing party.
- **Not yet built:**
  - portal reads of documents, invoices and commission statements;
  - Life and Health claim views for members;
  - the staff resolution of a party-match review (the task exists);
  - the portal Keycloak clients in the realm export (for the Keycloak batch).
- **A gap, not built:** party registration by staff has no duplicate check today; only portal registrations are matched.

## Tests
- **End to end (General):**
  - a broker sees its own client's policy and not the other's (list and ID), nor a claim on it, and quotes only for its own client, under its own code;
  - a member opens only their own policy, even with a guessed ID, and notifies a claim only on it;
  - refused: a staff endpoint, the staff client and a token without MFA;
  - a matching registration gets the same answer as a new one, plus a review task;
  - the broker may then quote for the client it registered.
- **End to end (Health):**
  - a provider submits as itself, and another provider can't read or list that claim;
  - a member sees only their own membership;
  - member MFA is refused once the portal's policy requires it.
- **Integration:** the link lifecycle (unknown subject, a second link, pending gives nothing, the maker cannot approve, the checker can, linked means portal only, ended at once).

### Request 46: arrears opened only on the second run
- **The cause.** An invoice issued after its instalment's due date took the issue date as its due date. When the run on the due date was missed, the next run invoiced the premium as due that same day, so it was not yet overdue and no case opened. Only the run after that found it overdue. A single run two days late failed the same way.
- **The fix:**
  - the daily run invoices a premium as due on the instalment's own date, so the first run after that date ages it, 1 day overdue;
  - back premiums of a policy billed for the first time (backdated issue, an endorsement's new schedule, reinstatement, Health enrolment) are still due from the day they are invoiced. Otherwise a backdated policy would be in arrears, and could lapse, the moment it was billed.
- **Without a bucket** ageing used to refuse (`NO_AGEING_BUCKET`), which the job only logged. Now:
  - an invoice passing its due date raises `billing.InvoiceOverdue`;
  - Billing's own consumer opens the case from it. When no bucket covers the days overdue, the delivery is parked (`ARREARS_AGEING_BUCKET_NOT_IN_FORCE`, with the days as item), and it is retried and the case opened once a bucket is published (ADR-022);
  - when a bucket exists, the run opens the case itself and the consumer finds it open.
- **Still refused, and logged by the job:** an account whose case is already open but has aged past the last bucket. This is a tenant setup gap like the one above, but its handling is not in this request; the tenant setup runbook should say that the buckets must cover every day to the last action.
- **Tests:**
  - **unit:** a late-invoiced premium is due on its own date; a first billing is due on the issue date; passing the due date raises `InvoiceOverdue` once;
  - **integration** (the test-only daily runner, as the front end used it):
    - with no bucket, the run the day after the due date marks the invoice overdue and parks the opening, naming 1 day;
    - once a bucket is published, the parked delivery is resolved and the case is open;
    - with the bucket, a single run the day after the due date opens the case at 1 day overdue, and the account is InArrears.

### GAP-048: Compliance retries
- `POST /compliance/returns` and `POST /compliance/report-runs` need `Idempotency-Key`.
- The six return actions need `If-Match` with the return's ETag (428 without, 412 when stale).
- Reads, creates and actions send an `ETag`. An action now answers with the new version; it used to give the one before the action.
- **Tests (end to end):**
  - a run without the key is refused, and a retried key gives the same run;
  - the ETag matches `rowVersion`;
  - an action without `If-Match`, or with a stale one, is refused;
  - each action answers with the ETag a fresh read gives.
  - The cross-tenant suite sends `If-Match` and still gets 404.

### GAP-047: report runs
- `GET /compliance/report-runs`: runs newest first, by report and by when they ran (`from` inclusive, `to` exclusive). It lists summaries, without results, up to 200 (default 50).
- `GET /compliance/report-runs/{id}/export`: the run's CSV, one line per metric (`report_code,from,to,metric,total`). It is served only for a run made with export, and only while the catalogue still lets your roles run and export the report. Otherwise `REPORT_NOT_EXPORTED` (409, new) or `REPORT_NOT_ALLOWED` (403).
  - The file is built from the run's stored result, so every download of a run is the same.
  - Each download is audited.
  - Text a spreadsheet would read as a formula is quoted.
- **Deferred:**
  - **Storing the export as a Document,** with retention and the run's `document_id` set. It needs a Documents category and retention rule for report exports, which are not specified.
  - **"The return's document":** what a regulatory return's document holds and its format are not specified.
  - **`FAILED`:** a run is computed in its request, so a refused or failing run returns an error and is not recorded. `FAILED` cannot occur today; it stays in the schema for runs made in the background later.
- **Tests (end to end):** the list (newest first, filtered), the CSV's type, name and content, and the refusal for a run made without export.
