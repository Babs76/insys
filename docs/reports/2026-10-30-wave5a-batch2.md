# Wave 5a batch 5a.2: complex cases, care plans and referrals

Branch `wave5a-2`, on `wave5a-1`. It builds the addendum's §4.3 to §4.5 (F034 to F038) with OQ-140, OQ-143, OQ-144 and addition 2.

## Scope
| Function | What |
|---|---|
| F034 | A complex case in Health (OQ-140), opened by a person from a membership, utilisation case or high-cost threshold. `OPEN`, `ACTIVE`, `ON_HOLD`, `CLOSED`; never closed automatically. |
| F035 | A case manager must hold `health_complex_case.manage`. Reassigning needs a reason. Every assignment is kept and audited. |
| F036 | One current care plan of goals and dated activities. A change needs a reason and keeps the earlier version. Coordination notes; one shared with another provider names the member's consent. |
| F037 | Progress on each item. Each open activity past its date raises one task, assigned to the case manager, while the case is active. |
| F038 | A referral: recorded by staff or by the provider itself, decided, its visits counted, expiring past its date. It affects no claim (OQ-144). |

## How clinical access works (OQ-143, addition 2)
- Views and lists hold no clinical detail. The clinical routes need a purpose, and every read and refusal is audited with no clinical content.
- A case's clinical detail is open to **its current case manager, by being assigned**, or to a holder of **the clinical function**, `health_case.read_clinical`. Writing it needs the same.
- So reassigning ends the previous manager's access at once, and it is tested. **Holding the case manager function grants no clinical access by itself.**

**Accepted as designed (Ade, 30 October 2026):** someone who also holds the clinical function keeps clinical access to every case after being reassigned, because that access comes from the function, not the assignment. The function means access to all cases, for tenants to grant sparingly. Recorded in the addendum.

## Differences from the addendum
- **A referral's reason (Ade, 30 October 2026).** Staff with the clinical function read it through an audited route. On the portal, the referring provider reads the reason it wrote, with a purpose. The receiving provider reads it only when the referral names the member's consent, and otherwise gets a 403. A third provider gets not found. Consent may be given when the referral is made or recorded later, and withdrawn; withdrawal ends the receiver's access at once (403), and both need a reason and are audited. These are staff routes.
- **Opening a case from a high-cost threshold** is a person's choice, with the source recorded. Nothing opens one automatically, as §4.3 says.
- **Consent** is a reference on the note or referral, not a consent record. Accepted for now (Ade, 30 October 2026); it is on the compliance list (§4.4).

## A guard against a portal route missing its declarations
My first version of the portal referral routes had neither `RequirePermission` nor `ForPortal`. They never reached main.
- **`RequirePermission`:** a guard already exists, `EveryApiEndpoint_DeclaresAPermissionPolicy_IAM007`. It did not miss this: it is an integration test, and the routes were fixed before it ran on them.
- **`ForPortal`:** nothing checked it. At runtime its absence fails closed: an external user is refused, so the route passes a staff test and fails a real provider.
- **New:** `EveryPortalRoute_DeclaresItsPermissionAndItsActors`, over every route under `/portal/`.

## Found by this batch's locked run
- **No one could be made a case manager.** `health_complex_case.manage` was checked when assigning a manager, but no function granted it, so every assignment was refused with `NOT_A_CASE_MANAGER`. There is now a function, `HEALTH_CASE_MANAGER` (under Complex case management). **Front end and tenant setup:** grant this function to the roles that manage cases.
- **`contactType` on a care note** now declares its values in the contract (BR-105).
- **The portal route guard** was proved both ways on a copy of the code: with `RequirePermission` and `ForPortal` removed, it fails and names the routes; with only `ForPortal` removed, it fails with "a portal route without ForPortal(<actor>)". `EveryApiEndpoint_DeclaresAPermissionPolicy_IAM007` fails on the first copy only.
- **Tenant setup with channels in force:** a provider who refers through the portal needs a channel rule allowing `health_referral.create`, and staff recording or withdrawing consent need one for `health_referral.consent`, as for any other transaction.
