# Wave 6 batch 6.1: exposure, tax and payables (F052, F094, F054)

## Tax (F094, OQ-058, OQ-156)
- A rate in `rating.cfg_premium_tax_and_levy_rates` that names a product applies to that product's invoices, by the issue date. A product with no such rate is unchanged.
- **The tax is taken out of the premium: amount x rate / (100 + rate). Totals don't change.** The addendum says only "from the existing rates".
- Finance posts the total as before, then moves the tax from premium income to the tax code's account (`PREMIUM_TAX`). A credit note takes its share of the tax, and credits add up to the invoice's tax exactly.
- A return totals credits less debits on the tax code's account for the period, so it equals the postings. A second person approves it. It exports as CSV, and INSYS files nothing.

## Decisions for Ade
1. **Tax-inclusive invoices.** I took the tax out because rating already adds tax into the premium when a product is rated with a jurisdiction: adding it on top would charge it twice, and customers have been quoted these totals. A product rated *without* tax would then show part of its premium as tax, understating income. *Recommendation: keep inclusive; finance to confirm per product (OQ-156), and set a product's rate only once its rating includes the tax.*
2. **One tax per product's invoices.** If two rates are in force, the lowest code applies. *Recommendation: enough until a product needs a levy on the invoice too.*

## Exposure monitoring (F052): nothing new built
The addendum proposed `reinsurance.cfg_exposure_limits` and `exposure_alert`. **General already does this** (F062, GAP-051, OQ-085): `general.cfg_aggregation_zones_and_limits` holds the limit and alert level, `general.aggregation_alert` records one alert a day from the snapshot, Workflow opens a review task, and `/general` lists them. Adding the tables would be a second set of limits for the same thing, and OQ-154 chose alert only. **My addendum was wrong on this point.** F052 is met by what exists; its task is the acknowledgement.

## Payables (F054): nothing new built
OQ-155 stops at payouts: the payout batches in Payments show what is due. F054 stays partial on purpose.

## Not tested here
A credit note's tax posting (`PREMIUM_TAX_CREDITED`) is tested in the domain but not end to end. Migrations and end-to-end tests run in CI.
