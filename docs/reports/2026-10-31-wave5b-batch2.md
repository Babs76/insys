# Wave 5b batch 5b.2: medication controls (F046, F047, F092, F093)

- **Limits:** `health.cfg_medication_limits`, per product or ingredient: a quantity or an amount per period, on top of category limits. The most restrictive wins. Use is counted from paid claims, so a reversal gives it back.
- **Duplicates:** the same ingredient while the last days' supply hasn't run out pends, under `DUPLICATE_THERAPY`. A refill is allowed once `ceil(days x (100 - tolerance) / 100)` days have passed: day 27 of 30 at the default 10% (OQ-151).
- **Restrictions:** a medicine flagged for prior authorisation pends without one, through the existing preauthorisation.

Two lines of one claim with the same ingredient and a days' supply also pend: the second counts the first as already dispensed.

Unit tests cover month and leap-year ends. Migrations and end-to-end run in CI.
