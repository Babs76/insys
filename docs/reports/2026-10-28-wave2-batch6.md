## 0. BR-095: a broker's portal quote is priced as it is made (group A, decided)

A broker's portal quote was never rated, so it stayed a Draft with no price: only staff could request the calculation.
- **How:** creating a quote through the broker portal, or any later version of such a quote, raises `GeneralQuotePricingRequested` with the rating inputs General gathers:
  - every attribute of the quote's risk objects, by name, as the rating factors read them (`source_attribute`);
  - the total sum insured as `sum_insured`.

  Rating consumes it, rates on the day the quote was made at an annual frequency, and records the calculation as one requested through the API would be (request reference `<quote number>-V<version>`). General attaches it from `PremiumCalculated` as before. The quote carries `pricedOnCreation`, which its later versions inherit.
- **When it cannot be priced:** Rating records nothing and publishes `PremiumNotCalculated` with its error code and reason. General keeps the quote Draft with `ratingIssue`. Two risk objects giving one input different values are not guessed between: General records `RATING_INPUTS_DIFFER` without asking Rating.
- **Referred:** as soon as such a quote is rated, the zone and peril limits (F062) are checked, as its issue checks them, and a quote over a limit is referred by the rules. Its referral has no referrer; it is decided under the tenant's underwriting authority (item 2). The portal view shows no premium while the quote is Referred or Declined.
- **The broker never sets the price:** no portal operation takes one; Rating's endpoints are refused to portal users.
- **Staff quotes** are unchanged: still priced by `POST /rating/calculations`.
- **Migration V0076:** `general.quote.priced_on_creation`, `rating_issue_code` and `rating_issue` (both or neither).
- **Tests:**
  - End to end, `GeneralProcessTests.ABrokersPortalQuote_IsPricedAsItIsMade_OrSaysWhyNot_BR095`. A broker quotes for its client on the broker portal, and the test checks:
    - the quote reads Rated at 3 050 (100 000 × 0.03 + 50), and Rating holds the calculation with the vehicle's group in its inputs;
    - the broker is refused Rating's calculation and the staff quote operations, and the price is unchanged;
    - a version 2 made by staff is priced at 6 050 with nobody asking;
    - a trailer without a vehicle group stays Draft with `RATING_INPUT_MISSING` naming `vehicle_group`, and no calculation is recorded;
    - a vehicle in a zone over its limit reads Referred with no premium and a referral with no referrer, which only an underwriter with the authority may approve (item 2); approved, the broker sees 3 050;
    - a staff quote stays Draft until staff rate it.
  - `GeneralTests.AQuotePricedAsItIsMade_AsksOnce_KeepsItsIssueWhileDraft_AndItsRulesReferralHasNoReferrer_BR095`: the event and its inputs, the flag inherited by version 2, the issue kept while Draft and cleared when rated, refusals once rated, and the rules referral decided by anyone.

## 1. BR-092: a broker registers and lists its clients' items (group A, decided)

The portal had no way to register a risk object or list a client's, so a broker could quote only on an item the insurer had registered and given it the reference of.
- **An owner (decided: option a):** `general.risk_object.owner_party_id` (migration V0077), on the staff view, the staff registration (optional, a party of the tenant) and the staff list filter. Staff set, change or clear it with `POST /general/risk-objects/{id}/owner` (If-Match). The change is audited with the owner before and after; the same owner again is refused.
- **Backfill, not guessed:** an existing item gets an owner only where every policy it is on has one policyholder and no quote for it names anyone else.
- **The portal:**
  - `POST /portal/general/risk-objects` registers an item for a client the broker services now, owned by that client;
  - `GET /portal/general/risk-objects?clientPartyId=` and `/{id}` read the items of a client the broker services now. Any other client, or its items, is 404.
- **"Services now" (decided: the broker loses the items when the client's intermediary changes):**
  - the client's live General policy (Issued or In force) is under one of the broker's codes;
  - with no live policy, the client's latest quote is;
  - with neither, the broker registered the client.

  General has no operation that moves a policy to another broker (the access spec names a General endorsement, which is not built), so the intermediary changes when the client's policy with one broker ends and another's is issued. Life's `INTERMEDIARY` alteration does not touch General items.
- **A scope fix that follows from it:** a portal quote takes only the client's own items, and only while the broker services the client now (403 `OUT_OF_SCOPE`, the same as for an unknown ID). Before, a broker could quote on any risk object whose ID it knew, and the quote's price then told it something about an item it had no relationship with. This is a breaking change for the portal. `ExternalUsers_…` now gives each client its own vehicle.
- **Known limit:** an item registered for a registration before staff linked it to an existing party stays owned by the registration; the registering broker keeps it through the registration. Items registered after the link belong to the existing party, as quotes are made for it.
- **Tests:**
  - End to end, `GeneralProcessTests.ABroker_RegistersAndListsItsClientsItems_AndLosesThemWhenTheClientMoves_BR092`:
    - broker A registers the client's car (owned by the client, no row version on the portal view) and quotes on it;
    - the insured vehicle staff registered without an owner is neither listed, read nor quoted on;
    - broker B gets 404 for the list and the item and 403 for a registration;
    - staff give the vehicle its owner (audited: owner after), the same owner again is refused, and broker A now lists both, paged one at a time;
    - the client's policy with A is cancelled and B's is issued: A gets 404 for the list and the item and 403 to register or quote on it, and B lists both and reads the car.
  - `RiskObjectOwnerMigrationTests` (V0077): an item on two policies of one policyholder (and quoted for them) is theirs; one on two policyholders' policies, one quoted for someone else, and one on no policy keep no owner.
  - `GeneralTests.ARiskObjectsOwner_IsSetOnRegistration_AndChangedOnlyToAnother_BR092`.
  - `OperatorTenantTests`, `FunctionAccessTests`, `EndpointSecuritySweepTests` and `CrossTenantSuiteTests` cover the four new operations.

## 2. A referral is decided under the tenant's underwriting authority (follow-up, Ade)

Ade asked that a referred portal quote be decided under the tenant's underwriting authority (type and amount), "like a staff quote's referral". Checking showed that no General referral was: any user holding `general_quote.underwrite` decided one, staff or portal, whatever the amount. The fix applies to all of them.
- **How:** `DecideReferral` reads the decider's effective roles (Identity) and the authority in force for them and the quote's product (Rules' `rules.cfg_authority_matrices`, as Life's underwriting decisions do). One row must cover the quote's total sum insured and list the decision code. Otherwise the decision is refused: 403 `GENERAL_UW_AUTHORITY_EXCEEDED`, and the quote stays Referred. The audit entry names the authority's role. General has no second-person route for a referral, unlike Life's, so the referral waits for someone who holds the authority.
- **Breaking for tenants:** General underwriters need authority rows before they can decide referrals. Recorded in the contract changes.
- **Tests:**
  - In `GeneralProcessTests` the senior underwriter's role may decide up to 500 000 and the underwriter's up to 50 000.
  - The BR-095 flow checks that the underwriter is refused on a 100 000 portal quote (amount), that the senior underwriter is refused a decision code outside its types (type), and that the quote stays Referred until the senior underwriter accepts it.
  - The existing zone limit and declined referral flows pass under the senior underwriter's authority.

## 4. A quote is priced in the frequency chosen, when its product allows it (follow-up, Ade)

Ade chose a tenant table of the frequencies each product allows (28 October 2026). INSYS had no such setting, and Rating gives one premium, which General takes as annual and Billing splits evenly.
- **Table:** `general.cfg_product_billing_frequencies` (migration V0078; catalogue addition; generated store and endpoints). The quote records the frequency chosen when the product allows it, with its periods per year (`premium_frequency`, `premium_periods`).
- **Views:** staff and portal quote views give `premiumFrequency` (the frequency, or `ANNUAL`) and `instalmentPremium`. The instalment is the annual premium over the periods, rounded to the cent as Billing rounds an instalment. `premium` stays annual.
- **Refusals:** an unknown frequency is 422 `GENERAL_CODE_NOT_IN_FORCE`; a frequency the product does not allow is shown as annual.
- **Not changed:** rating (one annual premium), and the policy's billing frequency, still chosen at issue.
- **Tests:** in the BR-095 end-to-end flow:
  - MONTHLY, allowed for the product, gives premium 3 050, `MONTHLY` and 254.17;
  - QUARTERLY, a billing frequency the product does not allow, gives 3 050 labelled `ANNUAL`;
  - a quote with no frequency is `ANNUAL` at 3 050;
  - WEEKLY is refused.
  - `StaticDataTests` and `MigrationTests` count the new table.

## 5. A referral reaches someone with the authority to decide it (Ade)

No General referral had a Workflow task: a referred quote waited in the quote list for an underwriter to notice it.
- **The task:** every referral now raises `GeneralQuoteReferred` with the quote's product and total sum insured. Workflow opens a `GENERAL_REFERRAL` task, due in one business day, in the queue of the role whose underwriting authority covers the product and sum insured with at least one decision type. Of several, it takes the smallest such authority (the most junior who may decide it), then the lowest role code.
- **Why one role, not several:** a task is in one queue. Any role whose authority covers the decision may still decide the referral (item 2's check), from the queue or not. With no covering role, the task goes to the queue configured for `DECIDE_REFERRAL`, or the default queue.
- **Closing:** the decision (`GeneralQuoteReferralDecided`) completes the task.
- **Readiness `GENERAL_REFERRAL_AUTHORITY`:** each sellable General product that no role's authority (with a decision type) covers is listed.
- **A defect found on the way (fixed):** `workflow.task.queue_code` held 20 characters, but a role code may have up to 80 (30 on an assignment). The routing test's role codes have 22, and its referral task could not be written; the aggregation review's tasks for a long role code had the same problem. Migration V0081 widens the column to 80.
- **Rules** gains `IUnderwritingAuthorities.ForProduct`, every role's authority for a product, and `ReferralRouting.RouteTo`.
- **Tests:**
  - The BR-095 flow checks the portal quote's referral task is in the senior underwriter role's queue (the underwriter's 50 000 does not cover 100 000), and is completed by the decision.
  - `OnboardingReadinessTests.AGeneralProductNoRoleMayDecideReferralsFor_IsListed_UntilOneMay`: with the ready tenant's authority retired, `P-READY` is listed; restored, ready again. The ready tenant's seed now holds an authority row.
  - `GeneralTests` checks the referral event carries the product and sum insured, and the decision event.

## 7. Billing's schedule totals the annual premium exactly (Ade)

Asked to confirm with a test, the test showed that it did not: each instalment was rounded on its own, so 3 050.00 billed monthly came to 3 050.04.
- **Fix:** a General policy's schedule keeps its annual premium (`billing.premium_schedule.annual_premium`, migration V0080). The instalments it generates total exactly their share of it, the last taking the rounding: eleven of 254.17 and one of 254.13. After an endorsement, the regenerated instalments total their share of the new annual premium. Fixed-instalment schedules (Life, Health, by hand) and schedules created before V0080 are unchanged.
- **Test:** `BillingTests.AMonthlyScheduleOfAnAnnualPremium_TotalsItExactly_TheLastInstalmentTakingTheRounding`:
  - twelve instalments total 3 050.00, eleven of 254.17 and one of 254.13;
  - amended from month 7 to 4 000.00, the six new instalments total 2 000.00 (the last 333.35);
  - a fixed-instalment schedule is unchanged.

## 8. BR-093: the perils and loss causes a member can report a loss under (group B)

- **What:** `GET /portal/general/policies/{id}/loss-codes` gives the member the perils in force today of the policy's product line, and those perils' loss causes, each with its code and label. Nothing else ties loss codes to a product: a peril names its line (`general.ref_peril_codes.line`) and a cause its peril. So the product's line (its class, or its family) picks them.
- **Scope:** the member's own policy only; any other is 404, as for the policy read. It is a read of the member's claims screen function.
- **Notifications take only these codes (decided by Ade; item 10).**
- **Test:** `GeneralProcessTests.AMember_ReadsTheLossCodesOfTheirPolicysLine_BR093`:
  - the motor policy lists perils ACC and THEFT_P with causes COLLISION and HIJACK, with their labels;
  - the property line's FLOOD and RIVER are not listed, and nor is the retired BREAK_IN;
  - another member's policy and an unknown ID are 404.

## 9. The instalment loading carries through to the policy and Billing (decided, Ade)

- **The policy** keeps the frequency and loading its quote was priced in. A policy issued in another frequency is refused: 422 `GENERAL_FREQUENCY_NOT_QUOTED`. Renewals keep the loading. The issue event carries the loading.
- **Billing** charges each instalment as quoted: the annual premium over the periods, with the loading. The schedule keeps the loaded annual premium, so the last instalment takes the rounding and the year totals it exactly, and it keeps the loading for endorsements (migration V0082).
- **The quote** shows `totalPayable` when a loading applies, staff and portal.
- **Test:** `GeneralProcessTests.ALoadedMonthlyQuote_IsBilledAsQuoted_TheYearTotallingTheLoadedPremium` takes a staff quote priced monthly at a 5% loading:
  - the quote shows 3 050 a year, 266.88 a month and 3 202.50 payable;
  - a quarterly policy is refused, and the monthly policy keeps the 5%;
  - Billing's schedule is eleven instalments of 266.88 and one of 266.82, totalling 3 202.50, and the first invoice is 266.88.

## 10. A loss is notified only with a peril and cause of the policy's line (decided, Ade)

- **What:** `NotifyLoss`, behind both the staff and the portal operation, refuses a peril that is not of the policy's product line, or a cause that is not of that peril: 422 `GENERAL_LOSS_CODE_NOT_FOR_LINE`.
- **Test:** in the BR-093 flow:
  - staff are refused FLOOD/RIVER (another line) and ACC/HIJACK (a cause of another peril), and THEFT_P/HIJACK is accepted;
  - the member is refused FLOOD/RIVER on the portal, and ACC/COLLISION is accepted.
  - Every other General flow's claims use the motor line's codes and pass.
