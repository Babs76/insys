## 0. BR-095: a broker's portal quote is priced as it is made (group A, decided)

A broker's portal quote was never rated, so it stayed a Draft with no price: only staff could request the calculation.
- **How:** creating a quote through the broker portal, or any later version of such a quote, raises `GeneralQuotePricingRequested` with the rating inputs General gathers:
  - every attribute of the quote's risk objects, by name, as the rating factors read them (`source_attribute`);
  - the total sum insured as `sum_insured`.

  Rating consumes it, rates on the day the quote was made at an annual frequency, and records the calculation as one requested through the API would be (request reference `<quote number>-V<version>`). General attaches it from `PremiumCalculated` as before. The quote carries `pricedOnCreation`, which its later versions inherit.
- **When it cannot be priced:** Rating records nothing and publishes `PremiumNotCalculated` with its error code and reason. General keeps the quote Draft with `ratingIssue`. Two risk objects giving one input different values are not guessed between: General records `RATING_INPUTS_DIFFER` without asking Rating.
- **Referred:** as soon as such a quote is rated, the zone and peril limits (F062) are checked, as its issue checks them, and a quote over a limit is referred by the rules. Its referral has no referrer, so any underwriter may decide it (BR-037). The portal view shows no premium while the quote is Referred or Declined.
- **The broker never sets the price:** no portal operation takes one; Rating's endpoints are refused to portal users.
- **Staff quotes** are unchanged: still priced by `POST /rating/calculations`.
- **Migration V0076:** `general.quote.priced_on_creation`, `rating_issue_code` and `rating_issue` (both or neither).
- **Tests:**
  - End to end, `GeneralProcessTests.ABrokersPortalQuote_IsPricedAsItIsMade_OrSaysWhyNot_BR095`. A broker quotes for its client on the broker portal, and the test checks:
    - the quote reads Rated at 3 050 (100 000 × 0.03 + 50), and Rating holds the calculation with the vehicle's group in its inputs;
    - the broker is refused Rating's calculation and the staff quote operations, and the price is unchanged;
    - a version 2 made by staff is priced at 6 050 with nobody asking;
    - a trailer without a vehicle group stays Draft with `RATING_INPUT_MISSING` naming `vehicle_group`, and no calculation is recorded;
    - a vehicle in a zone over its limit reads Referred with no premium and a referral with no referrer, which the same underwriter who works the quotes then approves, and the broker sees 3 050;
    - a staff quote stays Draft until staff rate it.
  - `GeneralTests.AQuotePricedAsItIsMade_AsksOnce_KeepsItsIssueWhileDraft_AndItsRulesReferralHasNoReferrer_BR095`: the event and its inputs, the flag inherited by version 2, the issue kept while Draft and cleared when rated, refusals once rated, and the rules referral decided by anyone.
