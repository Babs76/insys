## Defects found while answering request 53 (not fixed: your call)
A read of every approval type's rejection handling found records that rejection or expiry leaves stuck:
- **D-1 `PAYOUT_RELEASE`:** Payments has no handler for a rejection. The batch stays `Built`, and its payments keep their batch, so no later batch takes them: the refunds, claims and commissions in it cannot be paid.
- **D-2 `PROVIDER_PAYMENT_RUN`:** a rejection changes nothing; the run stays `Calculated` with no next step, and its claims stay assigned to it.
- **D-3 `BANK_ACCOUNT_VERIFICATION`:** adding the account superseded the party's previous account in that currency; a rejection does not restore it, so the party has no payable account until another is verified.
- **D-4 Expiry and withdrawal:** approvals expire after 14 days by default, and neither expiry nor withdrawal tells the owning module, so every "waiting" state above stays waiting.
- **D-5 Dropped rejections:** where a module's subject or maker check fails on a rejection, it only logs it; the approval shows `REJECTED` while the record still waits.
- **Proposed fix:** a rejected payout batch is cancelled and its payments released for the next batch; a rejected payment run returns to `CutOff` with its claims released; a rejected verification restores the superseded account; expiry and withdrawal are sent to modules as a rejection with the reason "expired" or "withdrawn"; a rejection is applied whatever the subject. Each with a test. About a day, in Payments, Health, Party and Workflow.
