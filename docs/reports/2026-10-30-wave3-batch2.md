## How I read the addendum where it left a choice (no decision needed unless you say otherwise)
- **"On by default" (OQ-123) and "existing tenants start off" (§5):** a tenant with no settings row and the LIFE entitlement is gated; V0092 writes a gates-off row for every tenant that exists today. The shared test tenants are seeded the same way, so existing flows are unchanged; the gate tests switch the gates on.
- **Working defaults for compliance to confirm:** LOW and MEDIUM are STANDARD, HIGH is ENHANCED; starter weights PEP 60, FATF call-for-action countries (KP, IR, MM) 60, an organisation 10, a Life relationship 10, a portal registration 10; bands LOW below 30, MEDIUM 30 to 59, HIGH 60 and above.
- **Policy loans** are refused at issue rather than held: a loan advance has no payout flow yet, so there is nothing to hold. Surrenders are not built (OQ-052). Say if you would rather loans were issued and their advance held.
- **Payout gate coverage:** claim payments (all domains), refunds and case redress. Commission is paid to intermediaries, and provider remittances to providers, who are parties screened when registered and monthly (§5's Health note).
- **Re-screen before a payout** happens when the payout is held, at most once a day per payee.
- **Additions beyond §9:** `POST .../due-diligence/unblock` and `POST .../beneficial-owners` (needed by §4.3 and §3); events `DueDiligenceChanged` (what releases held payouts), `DueDiligenceReferred` (what opens the hit review) and `PayeeScreeningRequested`.
- **Process codes:** `workflow.process_instance.process_code` widened from 20 to 30 so the signed-off name `DUE_DILIGENCE_HIT_REVIEW` fits.

## Found, not changed here
- **A query's audit entries are never saved (IAM-006).** Audit entries are written by the command pipeline; a query runs in a read-only transaction and its entries are dropped. The due diligence read is therefore a command, as Documents' portal content read already is. **Health's `HealthGetAuthorisationClinical` (`ReadClinical`, HLT-008) has the same gap today:** its reads of diagnosis codes are not in the audit trail. I propose fixing it the same way, with a test, as a small follow-up.
- **Occupation** is a risk factor in §4.4, but a party has no occupation field yet; the factor matches nothing (`TODO(spec)`).
- **Parties registered before V0092** have no due diligence record until something assesses them (a check, a held payout). Their tenants' gates are off, so nothing is stopped; a backfill can come with switching an existing tenant's gates on.

## Partitioned tables: the other seven (Ade, 29 October 2026)

Each column below was traced to the code that writes it, not just the schema.

| Table | Partitioned on | Kind | Set by | Beyond the partitions ahead? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `integration.external_call_log` | `called_at` | Creation timestamp | The partner adapters, with `clock.UtcNow` when the call is made (`StubPartnerAdapters.Call`), the only writer | No |
| `rating.rating_request` | `calculated_at` | Creation timestamp | `PremiumCalculation`, with `clock.UtcNow` from every caller: the calculation endpoint and the pricing a quote requests (`Calculations.cs`). The as-at rating date is a separate column, `rating_date`, and is not a partition key. | No |
| `rules.decision_log` | `evaluated_at` | Creation timestamp | `RuleDecision.EvaluateRuleSet`, with `clock.UtcNow` (`Decisions.cs`) | No |
| `document.document_access_log` | `accessed_at` | Creation timestamp | `AccessRecord.Create`, with `clock.UtcNow`, from the staff and portal content reads | No |
| `health.eligibility_decision` | `decided_at` | Creation timestamp | `EligibilityDecision.Record`, with `clock.UtcNow` (`CheckEligibility`); the service date the enquiry is about is not stored on it | No |
| `audit.audit_event` | `occurred_at` | Creation timestamp | The time the audited change was saved: `ModuleDbContext` takes `Clock.UtcNow` at save and carries it on `audit.SensitiveOperationRecorded`, so a delayed delivery still files it at the time it happened | No |

- **None of the seven is a business date.** Each takes the time the row is created, from the clock. A row can therefore never be dated later than now, and partition maintenance keeps 3 months beyond today (V0005, `Partitions:MonthsAhead`), with a health check below 2.
- **A past date** cannot fall outside the partitions either. The partitions start at January 2026 (V0003), and the audit time is the save time of a change made since.
- **The eighth and ninth tables** are `finance.journal` and `finance.subledger_entry`, partitioned on `accounting_date`, a business date. Wave 3 batch 1 dealt with them: V0089 creates their partitions when an accounting period opens.
