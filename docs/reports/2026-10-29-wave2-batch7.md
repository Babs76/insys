2. **BR-065, the claim's decision and the policy's cash value.**
   - The claim gives `approvalId`, `requestedBy`, `decidedBy`, `decidedAt`, `notifiedAt` and `reserveType`. On a read, `approvalId` is the approval waiting, or the one that decided the claim.
   - The policy gives `cashValue` and `cashValueDate`: the latest valuation stored on or before today. Nothing stores one until the actuarial basis (OQ-052) is defined, so both read null today. The test stores three valuations and checks that the latest past one is shown and the future one is not.
   - Tests: the death claim flow (`DeathClaim_…_LIF008`) and the loans test above.
3. **BR-074, underwriting cases.** `GET /life/underwriting-cases` lists cases by status, the underwriter assigned, and whether a decision waits for a second person. A case is in scope when its proposal is.
   - Test: the proposal-to-policy flow lists the case as pending, then as Decided.
4. **BR-075, BR-086, the proposal's policy.** The proposal gives `policyId` once converted. The policy list gives `proposalId` and filters by it. `LifeIssuePolicy` declares, in `x-eventual-effects`, that the proposal reads Converted once the issue event is handled.
   - Test: the proposal-to-policy flow.
5. **BR-076, who proposed a waiting decision.** The case gives `requestedBy`, `approvalId` (the waiting approval, on a read), `decidedAt` and `assignedTo`.
   - Test: the proposal-to-policy flow checks the proposer and the approval while the decision waits.
6. **BR-066, BR-077, BR-078, the Life contract.**
   - Each code field names its static data table (`x-code-list`): claim type, cause, beneficiary relationship, alteration, loan rate, reserve type, claim decision reason, underwriting decision and a decision term's exclusion, frequency, collection method and scheme type.
   - The limits the validators already enforced are stated in the contract: a case decision's reason (20) and code (15), and a claim's reserve type (15) and decision reason (20). The evidence type's limit (20) is stated too, but no static data table lists evidence types yet.
   - Member facts are the category rule's free inputs.
   - `x-eventual-effects` is declared on capture, submit, the case decision and issue, and on Rating's calculation and Rules' evaluation for a Life subject.
   - Rules' `subjectType` lists `LIFE_PROPOSAL` and `LIFE_UNDERWRITING_CASE`. Its `code` says a proposal's eligibility uses its product version's `eligibilityRulesetCode`, and a case takes any underwriting rule set in force.
   - Tests: the OpenAPI contract snapshot, and the documented-schema checks in the flows.

## Health

7. **BR-067, schemes.** `GET /health/schemes` lists schemes by number, sponsor and status. Schemes are not scoped by product or node, the same as their read.
8. **BR-068, BR-070, the membership.** The membership, accumulator and preauthorisation views give `currency`, the membership's. The membership gives its `events` (type, effective date, reason) in date order, `payerPartyId`, `billingAccountRef` and `currency`.
9. **BR-069, search.** `POST /health/memberships/search` takes the list's filters in the body, so a member number stays out of addresses and logs. It uses the same function and permission as the list.
10. **BR-072, the preauthorisation, eligibility and codes.**
    - The preauthorisation gives `clinicalReviewer` and `decidedBy`.
    - Its diagnosis codes are medical information (HLT-008), and the claim view already leaves them out. So they come only from `GET /health/preauthorisations/{id}/clinical`, which needs the clinical review permission and audits each read. This is the new action function `HEALTH_READ_AUTHORISATION_CLINICAL`.
    - `POST /health/eligibility-decisions/search` reads recorded eligibility decisions by member number and dependant, for memberships in scope, on the memberships screen.
    - Plan, option, dependant type, service type, member category, collection method, sponsor and diagnosis fields name their tables.
11. **BR-079, people and times.**
    - The medical claim gives `approvedBy` and `approvedAt`.
    - The payment run gives `approvalId`, `approvedBy`, `releasedAt`, and each remittance its advice `documentId`. Remittances are paid one by one, so the payout batch is the remittance's payment's (`paymentId`): a run has no single batch.
    - The utilisation and fraud cases give `openedAt` and `concludedAt`.
12. **BR-080.** `domain-model.json` has `ProviderPaymentRun` Calculated → Rejected (`RejectRun`). The lifecycle test no longer adds it by hand.

## Found while testing
- **Approval IDs were missing on reads.** A Life case and claim record their approval only once it is decided, so the reads gave no `approvalId` while the decision waited. The reads now take the waiting approval from Workflow.
- **A permission collision on the preauthorisation screen.** Putting the eligibility decisions read (`MembershipRead`) on the preauthorisations screen made that screen need `MembershipRead` too, so a user with only the preauthorisation read lost the screen (403). The read moved to the memberships screen, whose permission it shares. The other new reads share their screen's permission.

## Defect fixed: billing schedules starting late in a month (Billing, since Phase 3)
- **What was wrong:** each due date was stepped from the previous one, so a schedule starting on the 29th, 30th or 31st clamped to the 28th in February and stayed there. A monthly year starting on 30 October then had a thirteenth instalment on 28 October, and with an annual premium the last instalment was sized to 13/12 of it, so the year was overbilled.
- **How it was found:** CI passed yesterday on the 28th. Run today at the end-to-end clock (22:30 UTC, the 30th in Johannesburg), main at `c2a5870` fails `LifeProcessTests` (241 instalments instead of 240) and `GeneralProcessTests.ALoadedMonthlyQuote…` (13 instead of 12).
- **Fix:** each due date counts from the start (start + k periods). February takes its last day, and the schedule goes back to its day after.
- **Agreed behaviour:** unchanged. A year's instalments total the annual premium, as decided on 28 October, and now hold on any start date.
- **Existing schedules:** no data migration is needed before go-live.
- **Test:** `BillingTests.AMonthlyScheduleStartingOnThe30th_KeepsItsDay_AndHasTwelveInstalmentsAYear` (monthly and quarterly). The two end-to-end tests pass at this date.
