## 0. BR-091: portal lists (group A, added 28 October 2026)

Added by Ade ahead of the rest of phases 1 to 3, for the portal launch: the portals opened a member's Life policies, Health memberships and General claims only by ID.
- **New lists:** Life policies (member and broker), Health memberships (member), General claims (member and broker), and a broker's clients with one client by ID. General policies and Health claims were already listed; Life claims now page with a cursor like the rest.
- **Scope is the link only:** each query is filtered by the caller's party, or by its intermediary codes (with its sub-brokers' for a firm principal, from the distribution hierarchy), in the query itself so paging stays correct, and within the caller's F103 scope. No request parameter widens or narrows it.
- **A broker's clients:** a new building block contract, `IBrokeredClients`, answered by General (policyholders and quote proposers) and Life (policyholders and proposal applicants) under the broker's codes; Party adds the parties the broker registered. A registration linked to an existing party after review shows as that party, as the broker's quotes for it are made; anonymised parties are left out. The view is the name, kind and status only.
- **Test:** `PortalListTests.MembersAndBrokers_ListOnlyTheirOwnRecords_AndNeverAnothersByID_BR091`. Two members, a firm principal, its sub-broker and another broker, with records in General, Life, Health and Party. It checks:
  - every list, paged one record at a time, has exactly the caller's records;
  - the principal sees its sub-broker's, and the sub-broker does not see the principal's;
  - another's record by ID is 404;
  - the client view has only its four fields;
  - a member is refused the client list;
  - the same link keys in another tenant list nothing.

  The endpoint, function and operator sweeps pick the new operations up.

### Privacy check: insured lives and Health dependants (Ade, 28 October 2026)
Asked to confirm that an insured life who is not the policyholder, and a Health dependant, see only their own cover and claims. They did not, in three places (all from Wave 0b, made easier to reach by BR-091's lists):
- **The Life policy view** showed an insured life every benefit on the policy, every life's, with premiums, and the policyholder's party. It now shows them only the benefits on their own life, without premiums, and neither the policyholder nor the beneficiaries (`viewedAs: INSURED_LIFE`).
- **Life policy documents** were open to any insured life of the policy; the schedule shows the whole policy. They are now the policyholder's and the broker's only.
- **The Health membership view** showed a dependant the principal and every other dependant. It now shows them the plan and option, and their own entry only (`viewedAs: DEPENDANT`).
- **Already right:** a dependant's claims are only those where they are the patient (OQ-084); the principal's and other dependants' are 404. Life claims reach an insured life only as claimant. No portal view holds payer bank details.
- **Test:** `PortalListTests.AnInsuredLifeAndADependant_SeeOnlyTheirOwnCoverAndClaims_PrivacyCheck`. A policy with two lives and a beneficiary, and a membership with two dependants and a claim for each member. It checks:
  - the views, by ID and in the lists;
  - the documents relation;
  - each claim read.

## 2. The case's own single-person hold is retired (decided)

- **Removed:** `WorkflowPlaceCaseLegalHold`, `WorkflowReleaseCaseLegalHold`, their functions `CASE_PLACE_LEGAL_HOLD` and `CASE_RELEASE_LEGAL_HOLD` (gone from the catalogue and the data protection officer's template), and the case's `HoldForLegalReasons`.
- **A case is held by a record hold** with scope `CASE` (D-34), whose release a second person approves.
- **Migration V0068:** each case still under its own hold gets a record hold on it, placed by the person and at the time of its latest `LEGAL_HOLD_PLACED` event, with reason code `CASE_HOLD`; the case's flag is then cleared. Nothing held is released. The column stays, always false, and says so.
- **`CaseView.legalHold`** is read from the holds that keep the case (a record hold on it or on one of its documents), as disposal checks them.
- **Tests:** `CaseTests.ACasesOwnHold_BecomesARecordHold_AndTheCaseStaysHeld_V0068` runs the migration on a held case. The record hold has the original placer and time, the flag is cleared, the case reads as held and survives its retention while another is disposed of, and both old operations answer 404. `ARecordHoldOnTheCase_KeepsItPastItsRetention_UntilReleased_D34` (batch 4) still holds.

## 5. BR-030: withdrawing a Published version not yet Effective (decided)

- **The domain model** (`domain-model.json`) and the version's lifecycle gain Published → Withdrawn on WithdrawVersion. Retire stays Effective-only.
- **References:** a new contract, `IProductVersionReferences`, answered by General (quotes, proposals through their quote, policies), Life (proposals, policies) and Health (memberships) with their own SQL. Product Factory refuses the withdrawal while any references the version (409 `PRODUCT_VERSION_REFERENCED`), naming each.
- **The withdrawal** of a version that never came into force keeps its dates; its `ProductVersionWithdrawn` event carries its effective date as the end, so no new business starts on it.
- **Test:** `ProductVersionWithdrawalTests`: retire refused; a General quote on the version blocks the withdrawal, named in the detail; with the quote gone, the version is withdrawn.

## 7. BR-026: parties

- **A masked identifier number:**
  - **Display:** `maskedNumber` shows the last four characters (`•••• 9087`), and `••••` alone for a number of four or fewer, so it never shows a number whole.
  - **Storage:** migration V0069 adds `party_identifier.number_last4`, written when an identifier is added, as bank accounts do (DBA-014).
  - **Older identifiers:** those added before V0069 have none stored, since SQL cannot read the encrypted number. The party read takes their last four from the number, in memory, and returns only the masked form.
- **`PartyView.kind`** is the `PartyKind` enum, as on the summary.
- **Code lists:** each code field names its static data table (`x-code-list`), including on the requests.
  - The two field attributes from BR-025 move to BuildingBlocks.Domain, beside `OneOf`, because Party's data records are domain types. No module contract used them.
  - `CodeList` gains a `Filter` (`x-code-list-filter`) for a table of several lists: marital status is `list_code=MARITAL_STATUS`.
  - Five fields have no list to name; OQ-121 records them.
- **The search log's `from` and `to`** are described as instants.
- **Tests:**
  - `PartyIdentifierMaskTests`: a 13-digit and a 4-digit identifier, masked through the API, with neither number nor more than the last four in the response, and again after clearing the stored last four.
  - `OpenApiContractTests.PartyContract_DeclaresKindMaskedNumbersCodeListsAndInstants_BR026`.
