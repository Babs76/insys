## For Ade's decision (built as far as the model goes)
1. **Commission already paid to the outgoing broker is not recovered.** The domain model gives a paid entitlement no clawback ("Paid" has no transition). When the invoice's commission is already paid on a settled statement, the outgoing broker keeps the days after the change, and the incoming broker gets nothing for them (so the insurer does not pay twice). This is logged. **Options:**
   - **(a)** keep it so;
   - **(b)** allow a partial clawback of a paid entitlement, recovered on the outgoing broker's next statement, so the incoming broker is paid its share.
   
   **Recommended (b)**: it is what OQ-135 (a) means. It changes the commission lifecycle (`domain-model.json`), so it needs your agreement.
2. **"The new broker told the change is pending" (OQ-136).** INSYS holds no contact for an intermediary: Communications sends to parties, and the intermediary register has none. The pending change is on the policy and the endorsement for staff, who tell the broker. **Options:**
   - **(a)** keep it so;
   - **(b)** add a contact party to the intermediary register and send it a notice.
   
   **Recommended (b)**, as a small follow-up.

**Decided (30 October 2026):**
1. A tenant setting, backlog B-009: `RECOVER` (default) offsets the unearned part of paid commission against the outgoing broker's next statement and allocates it to the incoming broker, recording any amount it cannot offset as owed by the broker, visible to finance; `KEEP` is today's behaviour.
2. A contact party on the intermediary register, backlog B-010; once a change is approved, both the incoming and the outgoing broker are told.

Neither changes what 4.3c merges.

## Choices made in building it (none changes a signed-off decision)
- **The `INTERMEDIARY` endorsement type is the tenant's**, configured with premium method `NONE`, as other endorsement types are. Its code is fixed: it is how a broker of record change is recognised.
- **On a policy-count plan,** the incoming broker's first accrual on the policy counts as its first, so it earns the plan's per-policy amount.
- **The appointment letter is any document filed against the endorsement**, in a category the tenant chooses. No category is reserved for it.
