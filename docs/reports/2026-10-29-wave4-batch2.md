## Fixed first: BR-109 / GAP-112 (group A, front end's live due diligence run)
**Fixed in commit `01f349c`** (the front end's pin).

- **Cause.** Due diligence asks for the enhanced approval from its reassessment. When a provider's result was the last check (the identity verification callback), no person was acting, and the record named `Guid.Empty` as the requester. Workflow refuses an approval with no requester, so none was created. The record also noted the request as sent, so no later assessment asked again. The delivery did fail and retry, listed as `FAILING` in the parked deliveries view, until it was dead-lettered after about 43 minutes. It was never swallowed, but it was easy to miss, and it could not succeed.
- **Fix.**
  - An approval the platform raises with no person acting names the **system requester** (`MakerChecker.SystemMaker`, `00000000-0000-0000-0000-00000000005e`).
  - Any person with authority for the type, and with its permission, can decide it. Maker-checker is unchanged for approvals a person causes: the person whose check completes it is the maker and cannot approve it.
  - The approval view gains `raisedBySystem`.
  - Workflow opens no requester follow-up task for a system-raised approval. Party receives the ending, assesses again and asks again.
  - A request recorded with no requester before this fix is asked again at the party's next assessment (a details change, or any check or hit review recorded).
- **A failed request is never silent.** Workflow still refuses an approval with no requester, and the delivery fails: it is retried with back-off, listed at once in `GET /integration/parked-deliveries?status=Failing`, and dead-lettered after its attempts (ADR-022). Tested.
- **Sweep: approvals raised with no signed-in user.** I traced all 27 approval requests in 14 modules to the code that raises them. Only the enhanced due diligence approval can be raised with no person acting. It is raised from the identity verification callback, screening results, approval endings, payout re-screening and party changes, and all of them go through the same assessment, now fixed in one place. Every other request is raised by a command that refuses any caller but an identified user (`ActorType.User`), and none of those commands is sent from an event handler, callback or job. Redress, the one Workflow request, already refuses a request without a person.
- **Tests.**
  - `DueDiligenceTests.APep_WhoseProvidersCheckFinishesLast_GetsItsEnhancedApproval_RaisedByTheSystem_AndAPersonWithAuthorityApprovesIt_BR109`: the person checks first, then the provider's result. The approval is created with the system requester, no delivery fails, a person with authority approves it, and the party is cleared.
  - `DueDiligenceTests.AnApprovalRequestThatCannotBeCreated_FailsItsDelivery_AndIsListedForOperations_NotLost_BR109`: a request with no requester creates no approval. Its delivery is `FAILING 1` and listed for operations.
  - `EnhancedApprovalRequesterTests` (Party, 3): the system requests when no person acts, and any person may approve; the person who completed it cannot; a declined request is asked again.
  - `ApprovalTests.AnApprovalRaisedByThePlatform_IsApprovedByAnyPersonWithAuthority_AndNeverWithout_BR109` (Workflow).
- **Ade's decisions of 30 October 2026, built (`{COMMIT2}`):**
  1. **No contributor approves.** Nobody who contributed to the party's current assessment may give the enhanced approval, whatever order the results came in. A contributor is anyone who recorded a check or reviewed a hit since the party was last cleared, or ever if it never was. Workflow asks Party through a new contract (`IApprovalCheckerExclusions`). It refuses such a person with 403 `MAKER_CHECKER_SAME_PERSON` and leaves the approval off their list of approvals to decide. The requester is unchanged: the last person who acted, or the system requester when nobody did.
  2. **The system requester is reserved.** V0097 forbids a user account with its ID, so it can never be given a role or sign in. Request authentication never treats it as a user. Workflow refuses it as a checker (approve or reject, 403), and Party refuses it as the giver of an enhanced approval.
  3. **One open enhanced approval per party.** When a system-raised approval expires, Party asks again and exactly one is open. Workflow already treats a second request for a record with one waiting as a duplicate. V0097 adds a unique index (one `REQUESTED` approval per record), so this also holds when two requests arrive at once.

## Choices made in building it (none changes a signed-off decision)
- **Rules on request.** Rules gains a consumer for `RuleEvaluationRequested`. It records the decision as any evaluation does, and General attaches the `RuleDecisionRecorded` to the renewal, as Life does for proposals. A rule set not in force parks the delivery (`RULE_SET_NOT_IN_FORCE`) for the tenant to configure it.
- **Fraud.** No register of open fraud cases on a policy exists for General to read. The review counts the claims the Claims Framework referred for fraud (a new `IClaimFraudReferrals`) and refers the renewal to a person; it does not make it ineligible.
- **Product successor.** "On sale" means the product's latest version is on sale for the renewal period. A tenant mapping a withdrawn product to a successor is not modelled.
- **Renewal on the offered premium.** The renewed policy copies the risks and coverages, and the offered premium is spread over the coverages as the issue does. The renewal quote stays the rating record; it is not issued.
- **A renewal not offered by the period's end** (still referred, or ineligible) is not renewed automatically, and the policy expires as before. Acceptance, non-renewal with notice and lapse are 4.3.
- **The ready tenant decides every type.** The test seed's clerk now has authority for every declared type, with the approving permissions and their back-office channel rows; before, it had five.
- **No new permissions.** Reading renewals needs `general_policy.read`; deciding one needs `general_quote.underwrite` and authority, as a quote referral does.
