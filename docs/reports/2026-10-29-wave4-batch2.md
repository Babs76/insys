## Choices made in building it (none changes a signed-off decision)
- **Rules on request.** Rules gains a consumer for `RuleEvaluationRequested`. It records the decision as any evaluation does, and General attaches the `RuleDecisionRecorded` to the renewal, as Life does for proposals. A rule set not in force parks the delivery (`RULE_SET_NOT_IN_FORCE`) for the tenant to configure it.
- **Fraud.** No register of open fraud cases on a policy exists for General to read. The review counts the claims the Claims Framework referred for fraud (a new `IClaimFraudReferrals`) and refers the renewal to a person; it does not make it ineligible.
- **Product successor.** "On sale" means the product's latest version is on sale for the renewal period. A tenant mapping a withdrawn product to a successor is not modelled.
- **Renewal on the offered premium.** The renewed policy copies the risks and coverages, and the offered premium is spread over the coverages as the issue does. The renewal quote stays the rating record; it is not issued.
- **A renewal not offered by the period's end** (still referred, or ineligible) is not renewed automatically, and the policy expires as before. Acceptance, non-renewal with notice and lapse are 4.3.
- **No new permissions.** Reading renewals needs `general_policy.read`; deciding one needs `general_quote.underwrite` and authority, as a quote referral does.
