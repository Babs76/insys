## 1. Claim payouts follow their claim
- **Payments:** a claim payment, read by ID or by its instruction, is scoped by its claim's product and branch, through the Claims Framework, as the instruction is.
- **Payout batches:**
  - a batch shows only the payments in the caller's scope, with its count and total of those, and `partlyInScope`;
  - a batch with none in scope is not found;
  - building or releasing a batch needs every payment in it in scope;
  - a batch's approval is scoped when all its payments share one product and node, and otherwise is for checkers with unrestricted scope only, since it shows the total.
- **Commission payouts** stay outside branch scope, but reading them needs `commission_payout.read`. So do building and releasing a commission batch, since they show the payouts.
- **Tests:**
  - a branch-B user cannot see a branch-A claim payment through `/payments` or its payout batch (404), and a user covering branch A can (Life death claim, end to end);
  - a user without `commission_payout.read` cannot see a commission payment or its batch.
- **Not scoped:** payouts of other kinds, Health provider payments for example, stay unscoped, as before.

## 4. F062 aggregation limits (built)
- **Zone and peril limits:**
  - issuing a quote that would take a zone and peril past its limit refers it (`AGG_LIMIT`) instead, and it issues once an underwriter approves the referral;
  - exposure is live: the sum insured of issued and in-force policies' risks in the zone, across the tenant whatever the caller's scope;
  - an endorsement over a limit goes to approval as always, with the limit named in the approval's reason.
- **The alert:** `general.AggregationLimitApproached` is raised from the daily snapshot once `General:Aggregation:AlertAt` is set; unset (the default), nothing is raised. The alert is not tested yet, because no level is set.
- **Aggregate claims limits:** a settlement over what remains of the coverage's aggregate in the policy period (or policy year for `ANNUAL`) is refused with `AGGREGATE_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`. Automatic and on-request reinstatement are not defined (OQ-076), so every aggregate stops at the limit for now, which errs against overpaying.
- **Tests:**
  - **end to end:** a referred quote that issues after approval; the endorsement approval naming the limit; a settlement within a 40,000 aggregate;
  - **unit:** a settlement over the remaining aggregate is refused.
