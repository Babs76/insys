## GAP-043: what happened to a member with no matching limit
**They were accepted with no limit at all.** With no free cover limit, enrolment sets the member's evidence to "not required", whatever the cover amount. So every scheme member was accepted without underwriting evidence, at any cover, never sent for underwriting.

- **Why no limit ever matched:** `size_band` is an `int4range` column. The validator accepts interval notation, PostgreSQL stores it that way, and the store parsed only "50-199" or "200+". So no stored band could ever match.
- **The fix:** the store now reads the one format the validator and the database use, with inclusive and exclusive bounds and open ends.
- **No data migration:** the column is a range type, so existing rows are already in that format.

## Held claim payment, and the Life reserve question
- **The fix:** an automatic release that is refused no longer stops silently. That covers a General settlement and each payee of an approved Life claim, whether the reserve would go negative or the payee's account changed. Now:
  - the instruction stays `Approved` with `heldReason`, `heldDetail` and `heldAt` (V0034);
  - the claims role (`Claims:Payments:HeldReleaseRole`, default `CLAIMS_MANAGER`) gets a Workflow task;
  - a `ClaimPaymentReleaseHeld` log event drives a Cloud Eye alarm;
  - releasing the instruction clears the hold.
- **Should a Life death claim's reserve be set automatically from the sum assured at registration?** The specifications don't say. CLM-004 only says reserves are traceable movements, and nothing in the Life requirements sets one. I raised **OQ-079** with three options (automatic at registration, automatic at assessment, manual as today) and built none of them. Which role resolves a held payment is a TODO(spec) on the setting.
