# Wave 5b batch 5b.3: chronic registration (F083 to F085)

- **Registration:** requested by staff or a provider with a condition and note; decided by someone with `health_chronic.decide` and the clinical function, not the requester (OQ-150, SRS-002).
- **Benefits:** while approved and in force, a line whose diagnosis is on the condition's list, or whose medicine is entitled, draws on the condition's benefit category. A condition with no category set draws on nothing special.
- **Entitlement:** a monthly quantity per product or ingredient, counted by calendar month from paid claims. Above it the excess isn't paid and the line says `QUANTITY_CUT`.
- **Privacy:** the condition, diagnosis and note appear only in the audited clinical read. A decline's rights notice names no condition; it goes to the patient, or the principal for a minor. It reuses the `CLAIM_DECISION_RIGHTS` notice type and its merge fields, with the registration number in `claim_number` and an amount of 0.00: *its template's wording should be checked for this use.*

**Differs from the addendum:** no "used to date" column, since it is counted from claims.

**No review task:** the addendum sets a review date on a registration and a task only for programmes (§4.4), so a registration's `reviewOn` is recorded and nothing acts on it yet.
