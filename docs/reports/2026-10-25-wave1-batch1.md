## Requests 48–50 (completing Wave 0b)
- **GAP-050, a membership's product:**
  - `HealthMembershipView` gains `productCode` and `productVersion`.
  - Found while testing: the daily job that gives a membership its plan's product once the plan names one recorded no version. It now records the version on sale that day, as enrolment does.
- **GAP-052, every enforced permission in the contract:**
  - A handler's own permission checks are declared as `x-additional-permissions` `[{permission, when}]`: `commission_payout.read` on seven payment and payout-batch operations, and `bank_account.verify` and `party.update` on Workflow's approve and reject.
  - `x-permission` stays a single string, because the front end reads it as one.
  - The approval view gains `requiredPermission`.
  - `OpenApiReviewTests.EveryEnforcedPermission_IsDeclaredInTheContract_GAP052` scans the code for `HasPermission(...)` checks and for the permissions approval requests name, and fails for any not declared. It found all three before the declarations were added.
- **GAP-051, aggregation alerts:**
  - Each alert is recorded (V0050, `general.aggregation_alert`), once a day per zone, peril and currency, and listed at `GET /general/aggregation-alerts`. It gives zone, peril, exposure, limit, alert level, % used and date.
  - Workflow opens one `AGGREGATION_REVIEW` task per zone and peril for the underwriting role.
  - No underwriting role exists in the specifications, so the role is the setting `General:Aggregation:AlertRole` (`UNDERWRITING`), with **OQ-085** to confirm it.

## Business dates (ADR-025)
- **Fixed:**
  - The static data reads (`GET …/{entity}` and `…/{entity}/{code}`) defaulted `asAt` to the UTC date, while new versions start on the tenant's business date. Between 22:00 and 24:00 UTC (00:00–02:00 in Johannesburg), a version just activated was not yet in force.
  - Billing's manual `POST /billing/accounts/{id}/age` also aged on the UTC date.
  - Both now use `IBusinessDate`, as ADR-025 decision 1 requires.
- **Found, not fixed (a finding for you):**
  - Tests take "today" as the UTC date, while the product mixes business dates (policies, static data, Billing) with UTC dates (Analytics projects by the event's UTC day; Identity uses UTC under ADR-025 decision 3).
  - Between 22:00 and 24:00 UTC, three tests fail on `main` as well as on this branch: `HealthPlans_AreSoldAsProducts…`, `ProductPublication_FromDraftToEffective…` and `ArrearsAgeingTests.ACasePastItsLastBucket…`.
  - Moving the tests to the business date moved the failures to Analytics, Life, Compliance and F103 tests. A fix needs a decision on which Analytics dates are business dates. CI runs outside that window until then.
