## For Ade's decision (built as far as the addendum goes)
**How an accepted offer other than a discount on a renewal "becomes an endorsement".** §4.3 says an accepted offer becomes an endorsement mid-term, or changes the renewal's terms. Today a General endorsement attaches, detaches or changes a risk (its sum insured and coverages). Nothing adjusts the premium itself or the payment terms, so:
- a **discount** accepted mid-term (a cancellation request case) has no endorsement to become;
- **payment terms** are Billing's (the schedule's frequency), not an endorsement;
- **excess and cover changes** could be a `ChangeRisk` endorsement, but the offer's single `value` does not say which coverage or excess schedule changes.

As built, a discount on a renewal is applied automatically. Every other accepted offer is recorded on the case, and its owner makes the change by hand (an endorsement through the usual maker-checker, or a billing change), then closes the case. **Options:**
- **(a)** keep it so;
- **(b)** add a `PREMIUM_ADJUSTMENT` endorsement action (a per-cent change for the rest of the period, approved like any endorsement) for mid-term discounts, and a Billing frequency change for payment terms;
- **(c)** give offers structured terms (the coverage and excess schedule they change), so excess and cover offers create the endorsement themselves.

**Recommended: (b), with (c) later if tenants use excess and cover offers.** Nothing else depends on it, so I moved on.

## Choices made in building it (none changes a signed-off decision)
- **Retention figures count in the month they happened** (a case closed, an offer made or accepted): a cancellation case has no renewal date. Renewal figures count in the month of the renewal date, as §4.7 says.
- **A rate is a new field on a figure** (`rate`, 0 to 1, four decimals; null with nothing to divide by), so the UI never derives one.
- **An offer's authority is its role.** `authority_role` names the role whose holders may offer it; none means any holder of the retention function. No monetary limit is specified.
- **Cover reduced at renewal** (4.3a's open point) still cannot happen: a cover offer is made by hand (above).
