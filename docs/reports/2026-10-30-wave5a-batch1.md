# Wave 5a batch 5a.1: provider measures, quality reviews and statements

Branch `wave5a-1`. It builds the addendum's §4.1 and §4.2 (F028 to F030) with OQ-141 and OQ-142 as decided.

## Scope
| Function | What | Test |
|---|---|---|
| F028 | A monthly job works out six measures per provider from last month's claims and cases. A measure below its `min_claims`, or with none in force, is shown but not judged. A breach opens a task and changes nothing else. | Unit, end to end |
| F029 | Dispute and complaint counts come from Workflow's cases through a public query (a count only). A person records a score of 1 to 5 with a reason. | End to end |
| F030 | A statement is entered, or imported as CSV in one fixed layout, and matched against the provider's paid claims. Each exception is resolved by a person with a reason. It changes no payment. | Unit, end to end |

## Differences from the addendum
- **Queue.** The addendum put a review queue on each measure. Tasks route by task type everywhere else, so `REVIEW_MEASURE` does too, and the table has no queue column. The readiness warning for a measure's queue is dropped with it.
- **Providers do not see their measures** (OQ-141), so there is no portal route.

## For finance and compliance
- A statement line matches by claim number. "Paid" is a claim in status `PAID`; a reversed claim counts as not on our records.
- A statement's "not on statement" lines are the provider's paid claims with a service date in the period.
