## Answered
**Does any plan track a family-level deductible or co-payment?** No. `health.cfg_co_payment_and_deductible_rules` has no family setting: its `cap_basis` is `PER_LINE`, `PER_CLAIM` or `PER_YEAR`, and the engine tracks both on the patient's own year accumulator. `PER_FAMILY` exists only on limits, and a reversal already gives that pool back.

## What was built
- **Recoveries.** A reversed claim raises one, once. The provider contract's flag decides: set (default for a medical-scheme tenant), it is deducted from runs; not set, it is invoiced at once. A member's goes to a person.
- **Runs.** Oldest first, never below nil. A net of nil is an advice with no payment, and the balance carries forward. The approval's subject holds gross, offset and repaid. A rejected run gives its offsets back.
- **Uncollected.** After the tenant's period, a task for a person, who invoices or asks to write off. Written off needs a reason and a second person.
- **Reversal.** A recovery raised in error is reversed; an offset already taken is repaid in the next run.
- **Credit notes.** Second person, then a recovery. Provider portal too.
- **Disputes.** A dispute case linking a remittance, run or claim holds the recovery from offsets until the case closes. A provider may link only its own remittance. A case moves no money.
- **Also.** `recoveryStatus` is filled and the `TODO(spec)` is gone; `replacesClaimId` is shown both ways, in the portal too; a reversed claim restores its authorisation if still valid and used by nothing else; the remittance shows the original, the reversal, the new claim and the net.

## For the front end and tenant setup
Everything is in `docs/api/contract-changes.md`. A tenant must add the six recovery posting rules (blocking readiness), the recovery approval authorities (blocking, through the existing check), `cfg_provider_recovery_settings`, and the `PROVIDER_RECOVERY_NOTICE` type and template. `HEALTH_WRITE_OFF_RECOVERY` and `HEALTH_REVERSE_RECOVERY` are privileged and granted one by one.

## Decisions for Ade
1. **How a tenant is "a medical scheme".** Nothing recorded this, so `cfg_provider_recovery_settings.medicalScheme` is a tenant setting. *Recommendation: keep it, and have compliance confirm s59(3) and any time limit (already on the list).*
2. **Credit note posting.** You said credit notes credit `1300`. A credit note raises what the provider owes, so I debit `1300` and credit claims; the offset or write-off that follows credits it. *Recommendation: keep this. Finance can change the accounts in the rule without code.*
3. **Invoice settlement reclass.** A recovery's invoice posts no premium. When paid, the receipt credits receivables (`1100`), so `RECOVERY_SETTLED` debits `1100` and credits `1300`. *Recommendation: finance to confirm.*
4. **Findings always take a second person**, not only over a limit, because no limit is defined. *Recommendation: keep it.*
5. **Scope.** A provider's recoveries span products, so recovery and credit note screens need an unrestricted scope (F103). *Recommendation: keep it for now.*
6. **Member overpayments.** Either invoice or write off. Deducting from later reimbursements (OQ-145 option b) is not built. *Recommendation: leave it unless compliance prefers it.*
7. **The three unguarded declarations** (event mappers, process definitions, approval decision consumers). *Recommendation: add the first two as tests; they are small.*
8. **The regex timeout on identifier numbers** (`Parties.cs:597`, `PortalRegistration.cs:61`): a stalled machine can turn a valid request into a 500. *Recommendation: fix on its own small branch.*
