## B-010
- **Register.** `commission.mst_intermediary_register.contact_party_id` (V0103). `party_id` stays the party commission is paid to.
- **Notice.** Once a change is approved, whether it applies now or later, General raises `GeneralBrokerChangeApproved`. Distribution, which owns the register, requests a `BROKER_CHANGE_NOTICE` for each broker, with `broker_role` `INCOMING` or `OUTGOING`. Direct business has no outgoing broker, so only the incoming one is told.
- **No contact party.** The notice goes to the intermediary's `party_id`. With neither, Distribution logs a warning and no notice goes, and onboarding readiness lists the broker as an advisory warning, `INTERMEDIARY_NO_CONTACT` (Ade, 30 October 2026).
- **Test.** The end-to-end B-001 test: a change approved for a later date tells the incoming broker's contact party and the outgoing broker's own party at once.
