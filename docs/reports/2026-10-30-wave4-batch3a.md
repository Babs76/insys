## Choices made in building it (none changes a signed-off decision)
- **`422 NON_RENEWAL_NOTICE_TOO_SHORT` is not used.** §4.3 names it for a decision too late for its notice, and OQ-132 then chose (b), extending cover until the notice runs out. A late decision is therefore accepted and extends cover; nothing is refused.
- **`AUTOMATIC` keeps `auto_renew`.** An `AUTOMATIC` product renews only where `auto_renew` is true, as before. `ON_ACCEPTANCE` and `ON_PAYMENT` products renew once accepted or to be paid, whatever `auto_renew` says.
- **ON_PAYMENT renews first, then is paid.** The addendum says it "renews when the first renewal premium is paid, within grace after the end date". The renewal policy is issued at the period end, so the customer has cover in grace, and 4.1's grace cancels it from its start if it is not paid. The renewal then shows `Lapsed`.
- **"Cover reduced" is not yet a trigger for worse terms.** A renewal copies the policy's risks and coverages, so cover cannot fall at renewal today; it can once a retention offer changes cover (4.3b), which will set it.
- **The non-renewal's notice goes at the decision**, which is always at least its notice before cover ends (or cover is extended).
