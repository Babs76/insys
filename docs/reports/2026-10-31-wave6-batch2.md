# Wave 6 batch 6.2: register, data-subject requests, disclosures, retention (F057, F059, F060, F064)

- **Register (F057):** an entry names what enforces it (`enforced_by`). One that names nothing is a readiness warning. No new rules engine.
- **Data-subject requests (F059):** received by staff or the portal, due after the tenant's days (default 30). A person confirms identity, and a second person approves before an export leaves or a deletion runs. A deletion refused because records are kept is the answer, with its reason. An export leaves out `HEALTH_*` records unless the caller has the clinical function, and audits how many it withheld.
- **Disclosures (F060):** a step refuses with `DISCLOSURE_REQUIRED` until the texts in force at its point are acknowledged at their current version. With no text in force, a step runs as before.
- **Retention (F064):** the daily job finds dormant or deceased parties that nothing keeps any more, and asks a person once. Anonymising stays a person's act.

## Differs from the addendum
- **No `party.cfg_retention_rules`:** `party.cfg_record_retention_policies` (OQ-120) already does this; the check behind anonymising reads it.
- **Requests live in Party, not Compliance:** the anonymise check and the linked records are there.
- **An export lists records and the party's own details**, not each record's content. A fuller export needs a public query from each module. *See decision 1.*
- **Text coverage is a warning, not a blocker** as the addendum says. A blocker would stop every tenant that hasn't adopted disclosures, and a text is what adopts a point.

## Decisions for Ade
1. **How deep an access export goes.** Today: the party's details and a list of every linked record, with type, ID and closing date. POPIA s 23 may want the record's content. *Recommendation: compliance to say; about 3 to 5 days per module if needed.*
2. **Claim decision (OQ-157).** A person decides a claim with no step where the member can acknowledge, so nothing is gated there; the text can be read, and the existing notices carry the decision. *Recommendation: keep; compliance to confirm.*
3. **A dormant party never arises.** OQ-054's "no activity" trigger is still undefined, so only deceased parties reach the retention review until someone sets a party dormant. *Recommendation: define it with compliance.*
4. **A deletion anonymises the party and completes the request in one transaction**, two aggregates against the one-aggregate rule, so a request can't be marked done without the anonymising. *Recommendation: keep.*

## Not gated
Life and Health renewals, and quotes outside General. Only the steps listed in the contract changes are gated.
