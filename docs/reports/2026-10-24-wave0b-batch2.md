## F005: what is built
- **When it applies:** once a tenant has channel definitions; until then nothing changes.
- **Which requests:** every command of General, Life, Health and the Claims Framework, and building and releasing payout batches. Reads, static data and platform endpoints are never channel-scoped.
- **The channel** comes from the token's OAuth client (`azp`), through the new `client_ids` of `channel.cfg_channel_definitions` (V0042; static data and catalogue updated). A channel named in the request is never trusted.
- **The check,** after the permission, in one endpoint filter:
  1. the client must be on a channel in force, and the channel must enable the transaction (the command's permission code), else `CHANNEL_NOT_ENABLED`;
  2. the caller's roles must have an allowed row for the channel and transaction, else `CHANNEL_NOT_ALLOWED`;
  3. the product, where the command has one, must be enabled on the channel and allowed by those rows. This is checked where the F103 scope is checked, since the product is known only there; else `CHANNEL_NOT_ALLOWED`;
  4. a row that requires approval is satisfied only by a command with an approval flow (endorsement submission, settlement requests, claim payment instructions, payout batches), else `CHANNEL_APPROVAL_REQUIRED`.
- **Recorded:** the channel is stored on General quotes (and their policies), General and Life claims, and Health memberships (V0042). Life proposals record the request's channel.

## For your confirmation (OQ-082)
- **Several roles combine,** and an `allowed = false` row does not override another role's allowed row.
- **Approval** is needed only when every allowed row for the product requires it.
- **Health on a product-limited channel** is refused (OQ-080).
- **Life proposals** record the request's channel instead of the body's.

## For your sign-off (not built)
**OQ-080:** `docs/specs/addenda/health-plan-products.md`.
- Each Health plan names a Product Factory product of domain `HEALTH` (one product per plan, options as tiers).
- Enrolment needs that product on sale.
- Memberships and everything that follows them carry it, so product scopes and the shared catalogue cover Health.
- Plans without a product stay fail-safe, and `/health` reports them.

## Tests
