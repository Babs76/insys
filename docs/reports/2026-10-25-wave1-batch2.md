## Built (addenda/communications.md, signed off 25 October 2026)
- **The module:** Communications, schema `communication`, role `insys_communication` (V0051). Static data:
  - channels;
  - notification types;
  - templates per channel and language;
  - channel providers.
- **Queueing:**
  - one notification per trigger, type and recipient;
  - the language chain: party, then tenant default, then English;
  - missing type, provider or template parks (ADR-022);
  - marketing without consent, or opted out, is recorded and never sent.
- **Sending:**
  - per tenant, one locked notification per transaction;
  - consent checked again at sending;
  - the address read from Party, recorded only masked;
  - a retryable failure retried three times with back-off, then the next channel;
  - ends as UNDELIVERABLE (no address) or FAILED.
- **Channels:** email, SMS, push and WhatsApp behind `INotificationChannel`, as stubs (OQ-088). Built by an agent in its own worktree (66 tests).
- **Triggers (§6):** built by a second agent (42 mapping tests):
  - payment reminder and final notice;
  - renewal invitation and reminders (`reminder_days`, OQ-089);
  - General and Life claim status;
  - Health claim decisions and preauthorisations.

  New events: `GeneralClaimDeclined`, `GeneralRenewalReminderDue` and `MedicalClaimRejected`. `ArrearsDetected` now carries the payer and the action. Recipient queries per line of business: the policyholder, the claimant, or the patient (the principal for a dependant under 18).
- **Consent:**
  - Marketing types need a consent purpose; their templates must name the sender and the opt-out contact (POPIA s69(4)).
  - An opt-out withdraws consent in Party, a withdrawal in Party opts out, and a grant in Party clears the opt-out.
  - The customer exception is not used (OQ-087).
- **Delivery callbacks:** ADR-023's endpoint, signature and replay code moved to the building blocks and is shared with Payments; Payments' contract is unchanged.
- **Reads:**
  - staff, within F103 scope; a Health notice's text needs `medical_claim.read`;
  - members see their own sent notices and their preferences, every type listed, and their devices;
  - claim notices are logged on the claim.
- **Decisions applied:**
  - OQ-085: a per-limit `alert_role`, `UNDERWRITING` by default;
  - OQ-086 to OQ-090, as signed off.

## Independent API review
An agent that had not seen the work read only the OpenAPI documents and `contract-changes.md`.

**Fixed in this batch:**
- **Callbacks:**
  - they declared bearer security and their headers only in prose;
  - their tenant scoping was unstated;
  - issued secrets are now no-store.
- **Preferences:**
  - they did not list the types a member can opt out of;
  - the opt-in rule and the link to Party consent were undefined;
  - If-Match was missing.
- **Marketing rules:** they were absent from the static data operations (now stated from the validators).
- **Health text:** staff with `notification.read` could read the text of Health notices.
- **Smaller gaps:**
  - there was no device list;
  - there was no request time or source reference on notifications;
  - the portal listed queued and suppressed notices.

**Left, with reasons:**
- A notice's proof of consent (consent record ID) is not stored; the purpose is.
- STOP replies and marketing campaigns are OQ-094.
- A reason on staff preference changes is not taken; the change is audited.
- Payments' callback endpoint creation still takes no Idempotency-Key, because adding it would break the current contract. It now matches Communications in everything else.
- No GET for a claim's communications list.
