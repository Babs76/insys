## CI: the integration job in two shards
- `tools/integration-shards.py` places every integration class (except the sweeps, which have their own job) in one of two shards, heaviest first on the lighter shard, by a start-up cost per class, one per test and extra weight for the known slow classes. A new class always runs.
- `ci.yml` runs the shards as a matrix (`integration (1 of 2)`, `integration (2 of 2)`), each with its own PostgreSQL container; each first checks that every class is in exactly one shard.
- Checked locally: the shards list 128 and 141 tests, together the 269 of the whole job.
- First CI run of the split, on `09aa4c4` (run 40182766325): integration (1 of 2) 145 passed in 9 minutes, integration (2 of 2) 156 passed in 11 minutes (301 together, as before); unit-and-architecture 841 (831 passed, 10 skipped); sweeps-and-end-to-end 38 passed.

## OQ-098: privileged switch-off; switched-off jobs shown
- `TENANT_SWITCH_OFF_MONEY_JOBS` (privileged): needed to submit a `tenant-function-availability` version that switches off `BILLING_DAILY_JOBS` or `PAYMENTS_DAILY_JOBS` (403 `FUNCTION_NOT_GRANTED` otherwise). A role holding it is privileged (approval and MFA to assign). The rule is stated in the table's create and edit operations.
- `JOB_FUNCTIONS_OFF` in `GET /integration/deliveries/summary`, and the `job-functions` check in `/health` detail, name the JOB functions a tenant switched off. Only switches the tenant made are shown: a feature the tenant is not entitled to is not "off".

## Business dates (ADR-025)
- **Analytics** dates written premium and registered claims by the tenant's business date when the event occurred (claims incurred keep their movement date).
- **Existing projections need rebuilding:** yes, once per tenant after the release (`POST /analytics/projections/rebuild`; deployment runbook, release steps). Before, an event between 00:00 and 02:00 Johannesburg time counted on the day before; the rebuild dates logged events again from their occurred time in the tenant's zone.
- **Fixed test clock:** every test and test host judge "today" as one date per run (`TestClock.Today`; hosts get it as the test-only `BusinessDate:TestDate`, which the host refuses outside Development and Testing). 41 uses of the real clock's date in tests now read it. Hosts with a fixed instant of their own (`BusinessDateTests`, the General 01:30 Johannesburg case) leave it unset and exercise the conversion.
- **23:30 UTC boundary cases** (`BusinessDateTests`, at 23:30 UTC on 31 October): the business date per tenant; the debit order due and invoiced, posted to the new period after the old one is closed; and now Analytics, which counts a policy on 1 November for the Johannesburg tenant and on 31 October for the UTC tenant, and dates a fact logged by the UTC day again on rebuild.
- **Shown at any hour:** with `INSYS_TEST_DEFAULT_TIME_ZONE=Pacific/Kiritimati` (UTC+14, already tomorrow for most of the UTC day) the whole suite passes, as at 22:00 to 24:00 UTC with Johannesburg.
- **Found and fixed:** the build-time OpenAPI tool and the endpoint warm-up could build the endpoints at the same time; once it failed the build inside API versioning. The warm-up now does not run under the tool.
