# Addendum: default role templates (OQ-111)

**Signed off once its two fixes are made (25 October 2026, Ade): the search log only for audit roles (made), and a second person for releasing a legal hold (proposed below). Not seeded.**

Generated from `src/Modules/Identity/Insys.Identity.Api/RoleTemplates.cs` (published at `GET /api/v1/identity/role-templates`, BR-088) from the function catalogue as built; `RoleTemplateTests` checks every template against every segregation-of-duties rule and the privileged list. Optional starting points a tenant copies and adjusts: copying a template creates the tenant's own role (`iam.cfg_roles_and_permission_sets`) and its function grants (`iam.cfg_role_function_assignments`), which go through approval like any Tier 1 change. INSYS never changes a tenant's copy when a template changes.

- **Basis:** ADR-026 (a role holds functions), the function catalogue as built (705 functions), `INSYS_NAVIGATION.md` (sub-domains), the approval types and authority (`frontend-integration-guide.md` §3).
- **How a template is made:** the sub-domains it covers, every screen and action in them for its actor type, less JOB functions, privileged functions and named exclusions; then further functions, and the privileged functions it is meant to hold, named one by one. An action brings its screen. A checker template adds the Workflow approvals screen with approve and reject; what it may approve, and up to what amount, is its authority rows.
- **Separation of duties:** makers and checkers are separate templates, and no template holds both functions of any rule below (tested).
- **Audit read:** only the audit roles (`AUDITOR`, `COMPLIANCE_OFFICER`, `DATA_PROTECTION_OFFICER`, `HEALTH_AUDITOR`) hold a function that needs `audit_event.read`: the audit trail, a record's audit history and the party search log (tested). The tenant and platform administrators no longer read the audit trail.
- **Revised (25 October 2026)** after the first draft was not signed off: the claims and finance templates no longer hold both sides of a payment; only the finance manager verifies payee accounts; privileged functions moved to a data protection officer and an integration administrator; the operations analyst decides no approvals and discards nothing; the platform administrator is split.
- **Revised again (25 October 2026):** every operational template held the party search log, pulled in from `PARTIES`; only audit roles now hold a function that needs audit read. The Health auditor is approved. The Wave 2 case functions joined: complaints and enquiries for the customer service agent and the compliance officer (who also decides appeals, PPR 18.6), disputes and appeals for the claims templates, case legal holds for the data protection officer, case redress approved by the finance manager; recording redress and reopening a case are granted one by one.

## Segregation-of-duties rules

| Function A | Function B | Conflict | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `CLAIMS_DRAFT_INSTRUCTION` | `CLAIMS_RELEASE_INSTRUCTION` | BLOCK | Who prepares a claim payment does not release it |
| `PAYMENTS_BUILD_BATCH` | `PAYMENTS_RELEASE_BATCH` | BLOCK | Who builds a payout batch does not release it |
| `PARTY_ADD_BANK_ACCOUNT` | `PARTY_BANK_ACCOUNT_VERIFY` | BLOCK | Who adds a payee account does not verify it |

Approvals already need a second person (the checker is never the maker); these rules also keep one person from holding both sides of a payment.

## The privileged flag

Eight were flagged first; **the other 20 were approved (25 October 2026, Ade) and switched on with the fix for front-end request BR-013** (a new tenant has at least two administrators), since flagging `ASSIGN_ROLE` and the role-grant functions makes the tenant administrator a privileged role, which a tenant with one administrator could not have assigned. None is in an everyday template, and each is named where a template holds it.

## Who reads medical information and the search log (HLT-008)

**Decided (25 October 2026, Ade): a separate Health auditor.** HLT-008 restricts medical information by role, purpose and minimum necessary access, and the party search log shows who looked up whom. `AUDITOR` reads every staff screen except `HEALTH_CLAIMS`, `HEALTH_PREAUTHORISATIONS`, `HEALTH_FRAUD_CASES`, `HEALTH_UTILISATION_CASES`, `PARTY_SEARCH_LOG`; `HEALTH_AUDITOR` reads those with the audit trail.

## A second person for releasing a legal hold (OQ-111)

The data protection officer places and releases legal holds and disposes of documents, so one person could release a hold and destroy what it protected. **Decided (25 October 2026, Ade): an approval type, `LEGAL_HOLD_RELEASE`, not a segregation-of-duties rule.** Releasing a hold on documents (`DOCUMENTS_RELEASE_LEGAL_HOLD`) or on the party search log (`PARTY_RELEASE_SEARCH_LOG_HOLD`) is a request that a second person approves in Workflow; the hold stays in force until then, and a rejection, expiry or withdrawal leaves it in force. The compliance officer approves it.
