# Addendum: default role templates (OQ-111)

**Signed off once its two fixes are made (25 October 2026, Ade): the search log only for audit roles (made), and a second person for releasing a legal hold (proposed below). Not seeded.**

Generated by `tests/Insys.Testing/RoleTemplates.cs` from the function catalogue as built; `RoleTemplateTests` checks every template against every segregation-of-duties rule and the privileged list. Optional starting points a tenant copies and adjusts: copying a template creates the tenant's own role (`iam.cfg_roles_and_permission_sets`) and its function grants (`iam.cfg_role_function_assignments`), which go through approval like any Tier 1 change. INSYS never changes a tenant's copy when a template changes.

- **Basis:** ADR-026 (a role holds functions), the function catalogue as built (550 functions), `INSYS_NAVIGATION.md` (sub-domains), the approval types and authority (`frontend-integration-guide.md` §3).
- **How a template is made:** the sub-domains it covers, every screen and action in them for its actor type, less JOB functions, privileged functions and named exclusions; then further functions, and the privileged functions it is meant to hold, named one by one. An action brings its screen. A checker template adds the Workflow approvals screen with approve and reject; what it may approve, and up to what amount, is its authority rows.
- **Separation of duties:** makers and checkers are separate templates, and no template holds both functions of any rule below (tested).
- **Audit read:** only the audit roles (`AUDITOR`, `COMPLIANCE_OFFICER`, `DATA_PROTECTION_OFFICER`, `HEALTH_AUDITOR`) hold a function that needs `audit_event.read`: the audit trail, a record's audit history and the party search log (tested). The tenant and platform administrators no longer read the audit trail.
- **Revised (25 October 2026)** after the first draft was not signed off: the claims and finance templates no longer hold both sides of a payment; only the finance manager verifies payee accounts; privileged functions moved to a data protection officer and an integration administrator; the operations analyst decides no approvals and discards nothing; the platform administrator is split. Cases functions join after Wave 2 is built.
- **Revised again (25 October 2026):** every operational template held the party search log, pulled in from `PARTIES`; only audit roles now hold a function that needs audit read. The Health auditor is approved.

## Segregation-of-duties rules

| Function A | Function B | Conflict | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `CLAIMS_DRAFT_INSTRUCTION` | `CLAIMS_RELEASE_INSTRUCTION` | BLOCK | Who prepares a claim payment does not release it |
| `PAYMENTS_BUILD_BATCH` | `PAYMENTS_RELEASE_BATCH` | BLOCK | Who builds a payout batch does not release it |
| `PARTY_ADD_BANK_ACCOUNT` | `PARTY_BANK_ACCOUNT_VERIFY` | BLOCK | Who adds a payee account does not verify it |

Approvals already need a second person (the checker is never the maker); these rules also keep one person from holding both sides of a payment.

## The privileged flag

Assigning a role that holds a privileged function is itself an approval (`PRIVILEGED_ROLE`). Two functions were flagged; `TERMINATE_TENANT` is flagged as decided. **The other 20 are approved (25 October 2026, Ade) and are switched on in the same batch as the fix for front-end request BR-013 (a new tenant needs at least two administrators), not before:** flagging `ASSIGN_ROLE` and the role-grant functions makes the tenant administrator a privileged role, so a new tenant with one administrator could not assign anything. The templates already treat every one as privileged: none is in an everyday template, and each is named where a template holds it.

## Who reads medical information and the search log (HLT-008)

**Decided (25 October 2026, Ade): a separate Health auditor.** HLT-008 restricts medical information by role, purpose and minimum necessary access, and the party search log shows who looked up whom. `AUDITOR` reads every staff screen except `HEALTH_CLAIMS`, `HEALTH_PREAUTHORISATIONS`, `HEALTH_FRAUD_CASES`, `HEALTH_UTILISATION_CASES`, `PARTY_SEARCH_LOG`; `HEALTH_AUDITOR` reads those with the audit trail.

## Proposed: a second person for releasing a legal hold

The data protection officer places and releases legal holds and disposes of documents, so one person could release a hold and destroy what it protected. **Proposed: a new approval type, `LEGAL_HOLD_RELEASE`, not a segregation-of-duties rule.** Releasing a hold on documents (`DOCUMENTS_RELEASE_LEGAL_HOLD`) or on the party search log (`PARTY_RELEASE_SEARCH_LOG_HOLD`) becomes a request that a second person approves in Workflow; the hold stays in force until then, and a rejection, expiry or withdrawal leaves it in force (the approval endings rule). The checker is whoever has `LEGAL_HOLD_RELEASE` authority, suggested the compliance officer.
- **Why not a rule:** a rule that no role holds both releasing and disposing still lets one person release a hold with no one checking, and a tenant with one data protection officer could no longer release at all. An approval puts the second person on the release itself, whoever later disposes.
- **To build, once confirmed:** the release request and its approval in Documents and Party (with the approval endings and D-5 handling, and two rows in the approval endings table test), the approval kind, and the compliance officer approving it.
